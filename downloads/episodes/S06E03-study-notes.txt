英文梗概（76 words，含剧透）
Nacho negotiates protection for his father and agrees to surrender himself under a plan that shields Gus. Before the cartel leaders, he gives the required account but also claims responsibility for Hector's stroke. He then kills himself rather than submit to their intended punishment. Mike witnesses the outcome from a distance. Meanwhile, Jimmy and Kim continue preparing their Howard scheme, including access to his car, unaware of how close their world is to the cartel's violence.

关键词采用常见美式读法的宽式 IPA；/ɝ/、/ɚ/ 表示带 r 色彩的元音，/i/、/u/ 不另加长音符号。音标对应显示的词条形式。词性列出本页讲解的用法；部分词有其他读法或词性。

01. misdemeanor · 关键词 · 回看 00:19:05
美式 IPA：/ˌmɪsdəˈminɚ/
词性：noun
English definition: A criminal offense in a less serious category than a felony.
轻罪
字幕中出现：misdemeanor
美国法律用语；具体分类取决于适用法律，不能只按日常严重程度判断。
The charge was classified as a misdemeanor.
该项指控被归类为轻罪。
She asked whether the offense was a misdemeanor.
她询问该违法行为是否属于轻罪。
The report distinguishes misdemeanors from felonies.
报告区分了轻罪与重罪。

02. bail · 关键词 · 回看 00:19:58
美式 IPA：/beɪl/
词性：noun / verb
English definition: Conditional release before trial, sometimes secured by money; to secure someone's release in this way.
保释；保释金
字幕中出现：bail
常用 on bail 或 post bail；保释不表示无罪。
The defendant was released on bail.
被告获准保释。
The family helped him post bail.
家人帮助他缴纳保释金。
The judge considered the request for bail.
法官审议了保释申请。

03. discovery · 关键词 · 回看 00:19:20
美式 IPA：/dɪˈskʌvɚi/
词性：noun
English definition: Finding something previously unknown; in litigation, the process of obtaining and exchanging case information.
发现；诉讼中的证据开示
字幕中出现：discovery
法律义是审前依法获取对方相关信息的程序，区别于普通“发现”。
The parties exchanged documents during discovery.
双方在证据开示阶段交换了文件。
The lawyer requested more time for discovery.
律师申请延长证据开示时间。
The discovery of a leak changed our schedule.
发现漏水后，我们改变了日程。（普通义。）

04. assault · 关键词 · 回看 00:20:45
美式 IPA：/əˈsɔlt/
词性：noun / verb
English definition: A physical attack or, in some legal uses, conduct that creates fear of imminent harm; to attack.
袭击；人身攻击
字幕中出现：assault
日常义较宽；具体法律构成因辖区不同，不与 battery 简单等同。
The police investigated the alleged assault.
警方调查了涉嫌袭击事件。
The witness described an assault near the station.
证人描述了车站附近的一起袭击。
The lawyer reviewed the assault charge.
律师审查了袭击指控。

05. accomplice · 关键词 · 回看 00:21:57
美式 IPA：/əˈkɑmplɪs/
词性：noun
English definition: A person who helps someone else carry out a crime.
共犯；帮凶
字幕中出现：accomplices
可数名词；指协助实施违法行为的人。
The accomplice waited outside the building.
共犯在楼外等候。
Police were looking for a second accomplice.
警方正在寻找另一名共犯。
He denied being an accomplice to the fraud.
他否认自己是诈骗案的共犯。

06. privilege · 关键词 · 回看 00:21:35
美式 IPA：/ˈprɪvəlɪdʒ/
词性：noun
English definition: A special right or advantage; in law, a protection that can prevent compelled disclosure of certain communications.
法律保密特权；特殊权利或荣幸
字幕中出现：privilege
法律中可指受保护、不须披露的沟通；不同于一般 confidentiality。
Counsel raised a claim of privilege.
律师主张适用保密特权。
The conversation may be protected by privilege.
谈话可能受保密特权保护。
Access to the archive is a privilege.
查阅档案是一项特殊待遇。

07. confidentiality · 关键词 · 回看 00:32:36
美式 IPA：/ˌkɑnfəˌdɛnʃiˈæləti/
词性：noun
English definition: The duty or practice of keeping sensitive information from unauthorized disclosure.
保密性；保密义务
字幕中出现：confidentiality
与法律 privilege 区分；保密承诺不总等于可以拒绝作证。
Confidentiality is essential to this work.
保密对这项工作至关重要。
They signed a confidentiality agreement.
他们签署了保密协议。
The breach of confidentiality damaged trust.
泄密损害了信任。

08. audacious · 关键词 · 回看 00:17:20
美式 IPA：/ɔˈdeɪʃəs/
词性：adjective
English definition: Remarkably bold, especially in a way that challenges normal limits.
大胆到出人意料的；胆大妄为的
字幕中出现：audacious
可褒可贬，取决于说话者对风险和动机的评价。
It was an audacious attempt to change the market.
这是一次大胆改变市场的尝试。
The audacious lie surprised even his colleagues.
这个大胆的谎言连同事都吃惊。
She proposed an audacious rescue plan.
她提出了一项大胆的营救计划。

09. pseudonym · 关键词 · 回看 00:32:33
美式 IPA：/ˈsudəˌnɪm/
词性：noun
English definition: A name used in place of a person's real name.
化名；笔名
字幕中出现：pseudonym
用以代替真实姓名的名字；不必然涉及违法。
The author writes under a pseudonym.
作者使用笔名写作。
He used a pseudonym online.
他在网上使用化名。
The pseudonym concealed her identity.
化名隐藏了她的身份。

10. bad faith · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:21:35
词性：noun phrase
English definition: Dishonesty or an intention to mislead rather than deal fairly.
恶意；缺乏诚意
字幕中出现：bad faith
in bad faith 与 in good faith 相对；法律语境含义依具体规则。
They negotiated in bad faith.
他们在谈判中缺乏诚意。
The complaint alleged bad faith.
投诉指称对方存在恶意。
A disagreement alone does not prove bad faith.
仅有分歧不能证明恶意。
