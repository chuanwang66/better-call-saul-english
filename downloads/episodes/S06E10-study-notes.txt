风骚律师逐集英语学习册 · S06E10 Nippy
英文梗概（76 words，含剧透）
Living as Gene, Jimmy befriends Marion to gain leverage over her son Jeff, who has recognized him as Saul. Rather than flee, Gene recruits Jeff into a carefully rehearsed department-store robbery. He distracts a security guard while Jeff steals selected goods. When a mishap threatens the timing, Gene improvises an emotional diversion. Afterward, he uses their shared criminal exposure to demand silence. The successful operation awakens skills and appetites that his quiet Omaha life had suppressed.

重点词汇：9 项（单词与词组合计）

01. conspiracy · 关键词 · 回看 00:45:05
共谋；阴谋
字幕中出现：Conspiracy
法律语境强调共同约定实施违法行为；也用于非法律的秘密计划。
Prosecutors alleged a conspiracy to commit fraud.
检方指控存在诈骗共谋。
She denied taking part in the conspiracy.
她否认参与阴谋。
The conspiracy involved several companies.
共谋涉及几家公司。

02. blackmail · 关键词 · 回看 00:08:40
敲诈勒索；以曝光信息等相威胁
字幕中出现：blackmail
名词或动词；日常 emotional blackmail 指情感勒索。
She reported the blackmail attempt.
她举报了那次敲诈企图。
He refused to give in to blackmail.
他拒绝向敲诈低头。
Guilt can be used as a form of emotional blackmail.
内疚感可能被用作情感勒索的手段。

03. on the line · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:09:20
面临风险；处于通话中
字幕中出现：on the line
声誉、工作等 on the line 指可能失去；电话语境指在线。
My reputation is on the line.
我的声誉面临风险。
Is the client still on the line?
客户还在线上吗？
There's a lot of money on the line.
有一大笔钱面临风险。

字幕中出现：strong-arm
strong-arm someone into doing something；负面动词。
They tried to strong-arm her into signing.
他们试图威逼她签字。
The supplier strong-armed smaller firms.
供应商胁迫了较小的公司。
He refused to be strong-armed into silence.
他拒绝受威胁而保持沉默。

07. logistics · 关键词 · 回看 00:27:00
