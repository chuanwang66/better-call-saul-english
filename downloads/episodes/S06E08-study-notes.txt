风骚律师逐集英语学习册 · S06E08 Point and Shoot
英文梗概（76 words，含剧透）
Lalo uses Jimmy and Kim to draw Mike's security team away from Gus. Kim is sent to kill Gus, but reaches his men instead. Gus recognizes the diversion and goes to the unfinished laboratory, where Lalo records evidence of the secret construction. Gus uses his preparation and familiarity with the site to kill him. Mike manages the aftermath and supplies Jimmy and Kim with a cover story. Howard and Lalo are buried beneath the laboratory together.
