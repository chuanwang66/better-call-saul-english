风骚律师逐集英语学习册 · S04E08 Coushatta
英文梗概（73 words，含剧透）
Jimmy and Kim stage a campaign of letters and telephone calls from Huell's supposed hometown supporters. The invented community pressure persuades the prosecutor to reconsider the case. Energized by the success, they collaborate on another deception to change Mesa Verde's approved building plans. At the construction site, Werner's homesickness becomes harder to manage. The episode brings Jimmy and Kim closer through shared manipulation while showing how apparently contained schemes can encourage larger risks.

01. misdemeanor · 关键词 · 回看 00:23:43
轻罪
字幕中出现：misdemeanor
美国法律用语；具体分类取决于适用法律，不能只按日常严重程度判断。
The charge was classified as a misdemeanor.
该项指控被归类为轻罪。
She asked whether the offense was a misdemeanor.
她询问该违法行为是否属于轻罪。
The report distinguishes misdemeanors from felonies.
报告区分了轻罪与重罪。

02. probation · 关键词 · 回看 00:23:39
缓刑或社区监督；试用期
字幕中出现：probation
刑事含义按具体制度判断；工作语境可指试用期。
The sentence included a period of probation.
判决包含一段社区监督期。
She must comply with the conditions of probation.
她必须遵守监督条件。
My probation at the new job ends next month.
我新工作的试用期下个月结束。

03. defendant · 关键词 · 回看 00:30:16
被告
字幕中出现：defendant
可用于刑事或民事案件；不是所有案件里的被告都已被定罪。
The defendant entered the courtroom.
被告走进法庭。
The defendant denied the allegation.
被告否认了指控。
How many defendants are named in the case?
这个案件列了多少名被告？

04. discovery · 关键词 · 回看 00:24:24
发现；诉讼中的证据开示
字幕中出现：discovery
法律义是审前依法获取对方相关信息的程序，区别于普通“发现”。
The parties exchanged documents during discovery.
双方在证据开示阶段交换了文件。
The lawyer requested more time for discovery.
律师申请延长证据开示时间。
The discovery of a leak changed our schedule.
发现漏水后，我们改变了日程。（普通义。）

传票；以传票要求出庭或提交材料
字幕中出现：subpoena
法律名词也可作动词；不等同于 arrest warrant 逮捕令。
The witness received a subpoena.
证人收到了传票。
The court issued a subpoena for the records.
法院发出传票，要求提交记录。
The lawyer sought to subpoena another witness.
律师申请以传票传唤另一名证人。

银行调查了可能的欺诈行为。
The company introduced fraud prevention training.
公司开展了防欺诈培训。
She realized the offer was a fraud.
她意识到那个邀约是骗局。

The dispute led to years of litigation.
争议导致多年诉讼。

她从事商事诉讼工作。

08. contingent · 关键词 · 回看 00:29:54
取决于条件的；代表团
字幕中出现：contingent
形容词 contingent on/upon；名词 a contingent 指一支队伍或代表团。
本集用法：本集是名词：准备到场的一支支持者队伍或代表团。
The offer is contingent on approval.
这项要约以获批为条件。
Payment is contingent upon completion.
付款取决于是否完成。
A small contingent attended the hearing.
一小队代表参加了听证。

09. proffer · 关键词 · 回看 00:23:37
提出、呈交证据或陈述
字幕中出现：proffer
正式法律用语，也可泛指提出建议；保护范围依具体协议。
The prosecutor requested a proffer.
检察官要求提出相关证据或陈述。
Counsel made a proffer of the witness's testimony.
律师说明了证人预期证言的内容。
She proffered an alternative explanation.
她提出了另一种解释。

10. contempt · 关键词 · 回看 00:48:10
蔑视；藐视法庭
字幕中出现：contempt
法律搭配 contempt of court；普通语境可指强烈轻蔑。
The judge warned him about contempt of court.
法官警告他可能构成藐视法庭。
She spoke with contempt for the scheme.
她对这套把戏表示蔑视。
He was held in contempt after refusing the order.
他拒绝服从命令后被认定藐视法庭。

She works in the district attorney's office.
她在地方检察官办公室工作。

地方检察官审查了证据。

The court credited her with time served.
法院折抵了她已被羁押的时间。
The agreement recommended time served.
协议建议以已服刑时间处理。
