英文梗概（72 words，含剧透）
The Kettlemans offer Jimmy money to keep quiet, which he accepts as a supposed retainer. He uses it to copy Howard's appearance and advertise beside an HHM-like logo. The firm demands that the billboard come down. Jimmy then stages a dramatic rescue of a worker hanging from the sign, winning publicity and new enquiries. His success depends on manipulating the story, while Chuck remains unaware of how his brother obtained the attention.

重点词汇：9 项（单词与词组合计）
关键词采用常见美式读法的宽式 IPA；/ɝ/、/ɚ/ 表示带 r 色彩的元音，/i/、/u/ 不另加长音符号。音标对应显示的词条形式。词性列出本页讲解的用法；部分词有其他读法或词性。

01. retainer · 关键词 · 回看 00:10:36
美式 IPA：/rɪˈteɪnɚ/
词性：noun
English definition: A fee or arrangement securing a professional's services or availability, especially a lawyer's.
聘用律师等专业人士的预付费用；聘用安排
字幕中出现：retainer
具体如何计费取决于协议；不必然指已赚取的费用。
The client paid a retainer before work began.
客户在工作开始前支付了预付聘用费。
Ask what the retainer covers.
问清预付费用覆盖哪些服务。
The firm explained its retainer agreement.
事务所解释了聘用协议。

02. bribe · 关键词 · 回看 00:10:16
美式 IPA：/braɪb/
词性：noun / verb
English definition: Something offered to improperly influence a decision; to offer such an inducement.
贿赂；贿赂物；行贿
字幕中出现：bribe
可数名词或动词；不等于正常服务费或合法报酬。
The official refused the bribe.
那名官员拒绝了贿赂。
The employee was accused of accepting a bribe.
该员工被指控收受贿赂。
Nobody should have to bribe someone to receive a public service.
任何人都不应该靠行贿才能获得公共服务。

03. injunction · 关键词 · 回看 00:28:18
美式 IPA：/ɪnˈdʒʌŋkʃən/
词性：noun
English definition: A court order requiring someone to do or stop doing a specified act.
法院禁令
字幕中出现：injunction
常要求停止某行为，也可要求实施某行为；不是一般律师警告函。
The court issued an injunction.
法院发出了禁令。
They sought an injunction against construction.
他们申请禁令阻止施工。
Violating the injunction had serious consequences.
违反禁令产生了严重后果。

04. infringement · 关键词 · 回看 00:28:58
美式 IPA：/ɪnˈfrɪndʒmənt/
词性：noun
English definition: An unauthorized interference with a right, especially an intellectual property right.
侵权；侵犯
字幕中出现：infringement
infringement of a right；常见 copyright/trademark infringement。
The claim concerned trademark infringement.
诉求涉及商标侵权。
They denied any infringement of her rights.
他们否认侵犯了她的权利。
Counsel reviewed the alleged infringement.
律师审查了被指称的侵权行为。

05. predatory · 关键词 · 回看 00:31:34
美式 IPA：/ˈprɛdəˌtɔri/
词性：adjective
English definition: Seeking an advantage by exploiting people who are vulnerable.
掠夺性的；专门利用弱者的
字幕中出现：predatory
predatory lending/behaviour，带强烈负面评价。
The company used predatory pricing.
公司采用了掠夺性定价。
They warned about predatory lenders.
他们警告要提防掠夺性贷款机构。
The scheme exploited vulnerable people in a predatory way.
骗局以掠夺方式利用了弱势人群。

06. public defender · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:34:42
词性：noun phrase
English definition: A publicly funded lawyer who represents eligible defendants unable to afford counsel.
公设辩护律师
字幕中出现：public defender
美国语境常指为符合条件、无力聘律师的被告提供辩护的律师。
A public defender represented the defendant.
一名公设辩护律师代表被告。
She works as a public defender.
她担任公设辩护律师。
The public defender asked for more time to prepare.
公设辩护律师申请更多准备时间。

07. probable cause · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:15:50
词性：noun phrase
English definition: A reasonable basis, grounded in facts, for believing a crime occurred or evidence will be found.
相当理由；合理根据
字幕中出现：probable cause
美国刑事程序术语，常与搜查、逮捕相关；不是定罪标准。
The judge considered whether probable cause existed.
法官考虑是否存在相当理由。
The warrant application described probable cause.
搜查令申请陈述了合理根据。
Suspicion alone may not establish probable cause.
仅有怀疑可能不足以构成相当理由。

08. cease and desist · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:25:55
词性：verb phrase
English definition: To stop an activity and refrain from starting it again.
停止并不得继续某种行为
字幕中出现：cease and desist
常见 cease-and-desist letter；发出函件不等于法院已裁定违法。
The company sent a cease-and-desist letter.
公司发出了停止侵害函。
The notice demanded that they cease and desist.
通知要求他们停止相关行为。
She asked counsel to review the cease-and-desist demand.
她请律师审查停止侵害的要求。

09. good Samaritan · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:16:06
词性：noun phrase
English definition: A person who voluntarily helps someone in difficulty, especially a stranger.
善意帮助陌生人的人
字幕中出现：good samaritan
源于圣经故事；特定法律中的保护范围需另查。
A good Samaritan helped her change the tyre.
一位热心路人帮她换了轮胎。
Police thanked the good Samaritan.
警方感谢了这位热心人。
He was trying to be a good Samaritan.
他只是想当个助人为乐的好人。
