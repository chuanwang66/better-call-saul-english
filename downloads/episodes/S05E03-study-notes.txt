风骚律师逐集英语学习册 · S05E03 The Guy for This
英文梗概（75 words，含剧透）
Nacho brings Saul to Lalo, who wants legal help for Krazy-8. Saul arranges a relationship with the DEA that serves a cartel strategy rather than simply his client's immediate interests. Kim faces Everett Acker, a homeowner resisting Mesa Verde's plan to remove him from his property. Her attempts to connect with him reveal how differently they understand her role. Mike continues to act recklessly, while Saul's legal work becomes more deeply entangled with organized crime.

重点词汇：10 项（单词与词组合计）

01. prosecution · 关键词 · 回看 00:23:12
控方；起诉；检控
字幕中出现：prosecution
the prosecution 常指刑事案件中的控方。
The prosecution presented its evidence.
控方出示了证据。
The defense challenged the prosecution's account.
辩方质疑了控方的说法。
The file was sent for prosecution.
案卷被移送起诉。

02. waive · 关键词 · 回看 00:30:18
自愿放弃权利、要求或费用
字幕中出现：waived
waive a right/fee；与 wave 挥手区分。
She waived her right to remain silent.
她放弃了保持沉默的权利。
The bank waived the fee.
银行免除了费用。
They refused to waive the requirement.
他们拒绝免除这项要求。

03. contingent · 关键词 · 回看 00:31:49
取决于条件的；代表团
字幕中出现：contingent
形容词 contingent on/upon；名词 a contingent 指一支队伍或代表团。
The offer is contingent on approval.
这项要约以获批为条件。
Payment is contingent upon completion.
付款取决于是否完成。
A small contingent attended the hearing.
一小队代表参加了听证。

04. confidentiality · 关键词 · 回看 00:07:29
保密性；保密义务
字幕中出现：confidentiality
与法律 privilege 区分；保密承诺不总等于可以拒绝作证。
Confidentiality is essential to this work.
保密对这项工作至关重要。
They signed a confidentiality agreement.
他们签署了保密协议。
The breach of confidentiality damaged trust.
泄密损害了信任。

05. blowback · 关键词 · 回看 00:43:07
意外的负面反作用
字幕中出现：blowback
口语，常指政策、行动带来的反噬；不只是一般反馈。
The publicity campaign caused political blowback.
宣传活动引发了政治反噬。
They underestimated the blowback from the leak.
他们低估了泄密的负面后果。
We need a plan for possible blowback.
我们需要应对潜在反作用的方案。

06. adverse · 关键词 · 回看 00:37:35
不利的；负面的
字幕中出现：Adverse
常搭配 adverse effect、adverse ruling、adverse party。
The policy had adverse effects on small firms.
该政策对小企业产生了不利影响。
They appealed the adverse ruling.
他们对不利裁定提出上诉。
We must disclose adverse information.
我们必须披露不利信息。

07. stipulate · 关键词 · 回看 00:36:30
明确规定；同意某项事实
字幕中出现：stipulates
合同 stipulate that；诉讼中 stipulate to a fact 表示双方同意。
The contract stipulates a payment date.
合同明确规定了付款日期。
Both sides stipulated to the facts.
双方同意了相关事实。
They stipulated that changes must be written.
他们规定修改必须采用书面形式。

08. plea bargain · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:23:14
认罪协商；认罪协议
字幕中出现：plea bargain
常见于美国刑事司法；不是普通价格讨价还价。
The parties negotiated a plea bargain.
双方进行了认罪协商。
The judge reviewed the plea bargain.
法官审查了认罪协议。
The report explains how a plea bargain works.
报告解释了认罪协商如何运作。

09. water under the bridge · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:04:48
过去的事；已不再计较的旧事
字幕中出现：water under the bridge
通常作表语，劝人放下既往争执。
That argument is water under the bridge.
那次争论已经过去了。
Let's treat it as water under the bridge.
我们就把它当作过去的事吧。
For her, the dispute was not water under the bridge.
对她来说，争端还没有翻篇。

10. motion to dismiss · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:22:31
请求驳回案件或诉求的动议
字幕中出现：motion to dismiss
诉讼术语；提出动议不等于动议已经获准。
The defence filed a motion to dismiss.
辩方提交了驳回动议。
The court denied the motion to dismiss.
法院驳回了要求撤销案件的动议。
Counsel prepared a response to the motion to dismiss.
律师准备了对驳回动议的答复。
