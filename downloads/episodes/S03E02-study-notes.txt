风骚律师逐集英语学习册 · S03E02 Witness
英文梗概（72 words，含剧透）
Mike follows the tracking operation to Los Pollos Hermanos and asks Jimmy to observe the restaurant. Their investigation brings Gus Fring into view. Jimmy and Kim hire Francesca to help manage their office. After learning about Chuck's recording through Ernesto, Jimmy confronts his brother in anger, destroys the tape and forces his way into the house. Witnesses are waiting. His emotional reaction gives Chuck a stronger route to attack his professional standing.

重点词汇：8 项（单词与词组合计）

01. mitigate · 关键词 · 回看 00:40:38
减轻严重程度或损害
字幕中出现：mitigates
mitigate risk/damages；不表示彻底消除。
The team acted to mitigate the damage.
团队采取行动减轻损害。
Training can mitigate operational risk.
培训可以降低运营风险。
The judge considered mitigating circumstances.
法官考虑了可减轻处罚的情节。

02. confidentiality · 关键词 · 回看 00:34:40
保密性；保密义务
字幕中出现：confidentiality
与法律 privilege 区分；保密承诺不总等于可以拒绝作证。
Confidentiality is essential to this work.
保密对这项工作至关重要。
They signed a confidentiality agreement.
他们签署了保密协议。
The breach of confidentiality damaged trust.
泄密损害了信任。

03. confession · 关键词 · 回看 00:37:31
供认；坦白
字幕中出现：confession
confession to a crime；不自动意味着供述真实或可采。
The detective recorded the confession.
侦探录下了供词。
Her confession changed the investigation.
她的坦白改变了调查方向。
The defence questioned whether the confession was voluntary.
辩方质疑供述是否出于自愿。

04. off the hook · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:16:41
摆脱责任或困境；电话响个不停
字幕中出现：off the hook
let someone off the hook 放某人一马；ring off the hook 形容电话接连不断地响。
本集用法：与 ring 连用，形容电话接连不断地响；不是免于追究。
The apology didn't let him off the hook.
道歉并没有让他摆脱责任。
You're not off the hook for the bill.
你还得承担这笔账单。
The phones were ringing off the hook.
电话接连不断地响。

05. paralegal · 关键词 · 回看 00:16:14
律师助理；法律辅助人员
字幕中出现：paralegal
不等于有执业资格的律师；职能依地区和机构。
The paralegal organized the case files.
律师助理整理了案件档案。
She trained as a paralegal.
她接受了律师助理培训。
A paralegal helped prepare the documents.
律师助理协助准备了文件。

06. consent · 关键词 · 回看 00:40:27
同意；许可
字幕中出现：consent
可作名词或动词；informed consent 是知情同意。
They acted without her consent.
他们未经她同意就采取了行动。
He consented to the interview.
他同意接受采访。
The form asks for informed consent.
表格要求取得知情同意。

07. probative · 关键词 · 回看 00:40:50
有证明作用的
字幕中出现：probative
法律形容词，probative value 证明力；不等于仅与话题有关。
The judge considered the evidence's probative value.
法官考虑了证据的证明力。
The document was highly probative.
这份文件很有证明作用。
They questioned whether the detail was probative.
他们质疑这个细节是否有证明作用。

08. prejudicial · 关键词 · 回看 00:40:50
造成不利影响的；可能引发不公偏见的
字幕中出现：prejudicial
法律中 unfairly prejudicial 与 probative 相权衡；不是简单“不喜欢”。
The defence called the photograph unfairly prejudicial.
辩方认为照片会造成不公平的偏见。
The comment was prejudicial to the negotiations.
这条评论对谈判不利。
The court weighed probative value against prejudicial effect.
法院权衡了证明力和不利偏见影响。
