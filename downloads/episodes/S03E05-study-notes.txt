英文梗概（73 words，含剧透）
Jimmy's disciplinary hearing becomes a confrontation over credibility, family history and Chuck's claimed sensitivity to electricity. Kim questions the witnesses and develops the defence's account. Jimmy then reveals that a charged battery has been placed in Chuck's pocket without his noticing. The demonstration prompts an emotional outburst in which Chuck exposes his resentment and distrust of Jimmy. Jimmy avoids the outcome Chuck wanted, but the public victory deepens the personal damage between them.

01. subpoena · 关键词 · 回看 00:34:15
美式 IPA：/səˈpinə/
词性：noun / verb
English definition: A legal command to testify or produce specified material; to issue such a command.
传票；以传票要求出庭或提交材料
字幕中出现：subpoena
法律名词也可作动词；不等同于 arrest warrant 逮捕令。
The witness received a subpoena.
证人收到了传票。
The court issued a subpoena for the records.
法院发出传票，要求提交记录。
The lawyer sought to subpoena another witness.
律师申请以传票传唤另一名证人。

02. disbarment · 关键词 · 回看 00:16:32
美式 IPA：/dɪsˈbɑrmənt/
词性：noun
English definition: The removal of a lawyer's authorization to practice law.
取消律师执业资格
字幕中出现：disbarment

The misconduct could lead to disbarment.
不当行为可能导致吊销律师资格。
He faced disbarment after falsifying records.
他因伪造记录面临取消执业资格。
Disbarment ended her legal practice.
取消资格终结了她的律师执业。

03. corroborate · 关键词 · 回看 00:30:15

没有证人能佐证该主张。

调查人员寻找佐证证据。
