01. bail · 关键词 · 回看 00:50:45
美式 IPA：/beɪl/
词性：noun / verb
English definition: Conditional release before trial, sometimes secured by money; to secure someone's release in this way.
保释；保释金
字幕中出现：bail
常用 on bail 或 post bail；保释不表示无罪。
The defendant was released on bail.
被告获准保释。
The family helped him post bail.
家人帮助他缴纳保释金。
The judge considered the request for bail.
法官审议了保释申请。

02. affidavit · 关键词 · 回看 00:23:38
美式 IPA：/ˌæfəˈdeɪvɪt/
词性：noun
English definition: A written statement that its maker formally swears is true.
宣誓书；宣誓陈述
字幕中出现：affidavit
通常指经宣誓作出的书面事实陈述；不是普通便条或无条件的真实性证明。
She signed an affidavit describing what she saw.
她签署了描述所见情况的宣誓书。
The lawyer attached an affidavit to the filing.
律师在提交材料中附上了宣誓书。
Does the affidavit include the date of the incident?
宣誓书包含事件日期吗？

03. indispensable · 关键词 · 回看 00:00:41
美式 IPA：/ˌɪndɪˈspɛnsəbəl/
词性：adjective
English definition: So necessary that it cannot reasonably be done without.
不可或缺的
字幕中出现：indispensable
比 useful 更强；indispensable to/for。
Her knowledge is indispensable to the project.
她的知识对项目不可或缺。
A clear record is an indispensable tool.
清晰的记录是必不可少的工具。
No employee should be made entirely indispensable.
不应让工作完全离不开某一个员工。

04. district attorney · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:23:38
词性：noun phrase
English definition: A public official responsible for prosecuting crimes in a local jurisdiction in the United States.
地方检察官
字幕中出现：district attorney
美国地方刑事检控职务；具体职责随司法辖区而异。
The district attorney announced the charges.
地方检察官宣布了指控。
She works in the district attorney's office.
她在地方检察官办公室工作。
The district attorney reviewed the evidence.
地方检察官审查了证据。

05. scot-free · 关键词 · 回看 00:43:25
美式 IPA：/ˌskɑtˈfri/
词性：adverb / adjective
English definition: Without receiving punishment or suffering the expected consequences.
未受惩罚或损失地
字幕中出现：scot-free
常见 get off scot-free，通常含说话者的不满或惊讶。
He got off scot-free.
他没受惩罚就脱身了。
They shouldn't walk away scot-free.
他们不该毫无代价地离开。
She feared the organizer would escape scot-free.
她担心组织者会逃脱处罚。

06. breaking and entering · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:48:17
词性：noun phrase
English definition: Unlawfully entering a building, traditionally involving some form of force to gain entry.
破门或非法进入建筑的行为
字幕中出现：breaking and entering
法律定性依地区及具体事实；不是任何“进入”都构成该罪。
Police found evidence of breaking and entering.
警方发现了非法闯入的证据。
The charge involved breaking and entering.
指控涉及非法闯入建筑。
An unlocked door does not automatically settle a breaking-and-entering case.
门没锁并不能自动决定非法闯入案的结论。

07. barking up the wrong tree · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:48:28
词性：idiom
English definition: Pursuing the wrong explanation, person, or course of action.
找错对象或方向
字幕中出现：barking up the wrong tree
比喻追查、指责或寻求帮助时判断错了方向。
If you suspect the receptionist, you're barking up the wrong tree.
如果你怀疑前台，那就找错人了。
The investigators were barking up the wrong tree.
调查人员查错了方向。
You may be barking up the wrong tree with that theory.
你的理论可能指向了错误方向。

09. keep your nose clean · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:43:30
