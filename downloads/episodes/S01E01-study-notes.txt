Jimmy McGill struggles to earn a living as a public defender while caring for his brother Chuck, a brilliant lawyer who avoids electricity. Hoping to attract a wealthy client, Jimmy approaches the Kettlemans, who face an embezzlement investigation. He also challenges HHM over Chuck's place at the firm. A staged accident involving two skateboarders is supposed to generate business, but they target the wrong car. Jimmy follows them to a house and unexpectedly encounters Tuco Salamanca.

09. out of pocket · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:33:24
自掏腰包；蒙受钱财损失
字幕中出现：out of pocket
本项目采用费用义；美式其他口语含义需另看语境。
本集用法：本集谈的是让对方自掏腰包承担费用。
I paid the travel costs out of pocket.
我自掏腰包支付了差旅费。
The cancellation left us out of pocket.
取消安排让我们蒙受了经济损失。
Employees should not be out of pocket for approved expenses.
经批准的费用不应让员工自己承担。
