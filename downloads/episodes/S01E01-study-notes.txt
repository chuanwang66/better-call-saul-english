英文梗概（76 words，含剧透）
Jimmy McGill struggles to earn a living as a public defender while caring for his brother Chuck, a brilliant lawyer who avoids electricity. Hoping to attract a wealthy client, Jimmy approaches the Kettlemans, who face an embezzlement investigation. He also challenges HHM over Chuck's place at the firm. A staged accident involving two skateboarders is supposed to generate business, but they target the wrong car. Jimmy follows them to a house and unexpectedly encounters Tuco Salamanca.

01. embezzlement · 关键词 · 回看 00:46:02
美式 IPA：/ɪmˈbɛzəlmənt/
词性：noun
English definition: The dishonest taking of money or property entrusted to one's care.
侵占；挪用受托管理的财产
字幕中出现：embezzlement
常见于雇员或管理者挪用经手资金的语境；区别于泛指 theft。
The accountant was accused of embezzlement.
会计被指控侵占受托财产。
The audit uncovered signs of embezzlement.
审计发现了挪用资金的迹象。
The report examined an embezzlement case.
报告研究了一起侵占财产案件。

02. felony · 关键词 · 回看 00:49:03
美式 IPA：/ˈfɛləni/
词性：noun
English definition: A serious category of crime, generally carrying more severe penalties than a misdemeanor.
重罪
字幕中出现：felony
美国刑事分类用语；具体定义和后果依司法辖区及法律而定。
He was charged with a felony.
他被控犯有重罪。
The form asks about felony convictions.
表格询问是否有重罪定罪记录。
The lawyer explained the difference between a felony and a misdemeanor.
律师解释了重罪和轻罪的区别。

03. defendant · 关键词 · 回看 00:12:27

被告
字幕中出现：Defendant
可用于刑事或民事案件；不是所有案件里的被告都已被定罪。
The defendant entered the courtroom.
被告走进法庭。
The defendant denied the allegation.
被告否认了指控。
How many defendants are named in the case?
这个案件列了多少名被告？

控方；起诉；检控

The prosecution presented its evidence.
控方出示了证据。
The defense challenged the prosecution's account.

The file was sent for prosecution.
案卷被移送起诉。

05. trespassing · 关键词 · 回看 00:09:49
美式 IPA：/ˈtrɛsˌpæsɪŋ/
词性：noun / verb (-ing form)
English definition: Entering or remaining on another person's property without permission; the -ing form of trespass.
原形：trespass
擅自进入或逗留他人土地、场所
字幕中出现：trespassing
常见标牌 No trespassing；不是泛指任何闯祸。
The sign says No trespassing.
牌子上写着禁止擅入。
They were warned about trespassing on private land.
他们被警告不得擅入私人土地。
The complaint concerned trespassing, not theft.
投诉涉及擅自侵入，而非盗窃。

06. arraignment · 关键词 · 回看 00:35:28
美式 IPA：/əˈreɪnmənt/
词性：noun
English definition: A court appearance at which charges are stated and the defendant enters a plea.
正式提审并要求被告答辩的程序
字幕中出现：arraignments
美国刑事程序术语；不是完整审判，也不等于定罪。
The arraignment was scheduled for Monday.
提审安排在周一。
She entered a not-guilty plea at arraignment.
她在提审时作了无罪答辩。
Counsel met the defendant before the arraignment.
律师在提审前见了被告。

07. discrepancy · 关键词 · 回看 00:16:11

English definition: A difference between things that should agree or match.

There is a discrepancy between the two invoices.
两张发票存在差异。
The audit found several discrepancies.

你能解释这个出入吗？

08. adversarial · 关键词 · 回看 00:38:35
美式 IPA：/ˌædvɚˈsɛriəl/
词性：adjective
English definition: Involving opposing sides that challenge each other's claims.
对抗性的；双方交锋的
字幕中出现：adversarial
adversarial system 可指由对立双方提出论证的诉讼制度。
The interview became increasingly adversarial.
访谈变得越来越针锋相对。
They adopted a less adversarial approach.
他们采取了较少对抗的方式。
An adversarial hearing tests competing claims.
对抗式听证检验相互竞争的主张。

09. out of pocket · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:33:24
词性：adverb phrase
English definition: Paid using a person's own money rather than reimbursed or covered by someone else.
自掏腰包；蒙受钱财损失
字幕中出现：out of pocket
本项目采用费用义；美式其他口语含义需另看语境。
本集用法：本集谈的是让对方自掏腰包承担费用。
I paid the travel costs out of pocket.
我自掏腰包支付了差旅费。
The cancellation left us out of pocket.
取消安排让我们蒙受了经济损失。
Employees should not be out of pocket for approved expenses.
经批准的费用不应让员工自己承担。
