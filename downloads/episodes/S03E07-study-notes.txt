01. malpractice · 关键词 · 回看 00:05:11
美式 IPA：/mælˈpræktɪs/
词性：noun
English definition: Professional negligence or misconduct that harms a client or patient.
专业失当；专业过失
字幕中出现：malpractice
常见 medical/legal malpractice；不局限于医生。
The client filed a malpractice claim.
客户提出了专业过失索赔。
Her policy covers legal malpractice.
她的保单承保律师专业过失。
An unfavourable result alone does not prove malpractice.
结果不利本身并不能证明专业失当。

02. suspension · 关键词 · 回看 00:05:09
美式 IPA：/səˈspɛnʃən/
词性：noun
English definition: A temporary removal from a role, activity, or professional practice.
暂停执业、职务或活动
字幕中出现：suspended
本学习册主要关注律师暂停执业；suspended 是相关形容词或过去分词。
The panel imposed a six-month suspension.
小组决定暂停其资格六个月。
She appealed the suspension of her licence.
她对暂停执照提出上诉。
He returned to practice after the suspension.
暂停期结束后他恢复执业。

03. due diligence · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:23:54
词性：noun phrase
English definition: Careful checking of facts, risks, and obligations before making a decision or completing a transaction.
尽职调查；应有的审慎核查
字幕中出现：due diligence
商务常指交易前查验信息；法律中也可强调合理审慎。
We completed due diligence before buying the business.
收购企业前我们完成了尽职调查。
The investor requested a due diligence report.
投资者要求一份尽职调查报告。
Don't skip due diligence just because the price is low.
别因为价格低就跳过尽职调查。

04. bar hearing · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:50:52
词性：noun phrase
English definition: A proceeding before a legal professional body concerning a lawyer's fitness, conduct, or right to practice.
律师执业纪律听证
字幕中出现：bar hearing
bar 在此指法律职业及其管理体系；不是酒吧或普通庭审。
The bar hearing examined professional misconduct.
律师纪律听证审查了职业不当行为。
He prepared witnesses for the bar hearing.
他为律师纪律听证准备证人。
A bar hearing can affect a lawyer's licence.
律师纪律听证可能影响律师的执照。

05. waiver · 关键词 · 回看 00:01:57
美式 IPA：/ˈweɪvɚ/
词性：noun
English definition: An intentional surrender of a right, or a document or decision recording that surrender or an exemption.
放弃权利的声明；豁免
字幕中出现：waiver
名词；sign a waiver 常指签署责任或权利放弃文件。
They asked visitors to sign a waiver.
他们要求访客签署权利放弃声明。
The waiver covered certain risks.
声明涵盖了一些风险。
A waiver does not resolve every liability question.
豁免声明不能解决所有责任问题。

06. reimburse · 关键词 · 回看 00:18:15
美式 IPA：/ˌriɪmˈbɝs/
词性：verb
English definition: To repay someone for money they spent on another person's or organization's behalf.
报销；偿还垫付费用
字幕中出现：reimburse
reimburse someone for expenses；与 compensate 损害补偿区分。
The firm reimbursed her for travel.
公司报销了她的差旅费用。
Keep the receipts to be reimbursed.
保留收据以便报销。
They agreed to reimburse the deposit.
他们同意退还垫付款。

07. acquisition · 关键词 · 回看 00:26:43
美式 IPA：/ˌækwəˈzɪʃən/
词性：noun
English definition: The act of obtaining something, especially buying another business.
收购；取得
字幕中出现：acquisition
商务中常指企业收购，也可指知识或资产的获得。
The acquisition expanded the firm's reach.
这次收购扩大了公司的业务范围。
They reviewed the acquisition costs.
他们核查了收购成本。
Language acquisition takes sustained exposure.
语言习得需要持续接触。

08. full disclosure · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:23:49
词性：noun phrase
English definition: The complete sharing of relevant information, including facts that could affect another person's decision.
完整披露；事先坦白
字幕中出现：full disclosure
法律或商务中指充分提供相关信息；口语常用于承认利益或背景。
Full disclosure: I worked for that company.
先坦白说，我为那家公司工作过。
The agreement requires full disclosure of fees.
协议要求完整披露收费。
Full disclosure would have changed their decision.
如果充分披露，他们会改变决定。
