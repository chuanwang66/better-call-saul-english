风骚律师逐集英语学习册 · S01E08 Rico
英文梗概（73 words，含剧透）
While helping an elderly client, Jimmy notices suspicious charges at Sandpiper Crossing. He investigates the billing, retrieves shredded documents and discovers a possible pattern of financial exploitation. Chuck becomes absorbed in the legal work and helps develop a substantial case. Their collaboration gives Jimmy hope that they can finally work as equals. As the case grows beyond a solo practice, the need for resources creates a new question about who will control it.

重点词汇：14 项（单词与词组合计）

01. discovery · 关键词 · 回看 00:43:03
发现；诉讼中的证据开示
字幕中出现：discovery
法律义是审前依法获取对方相关信息的程序，区别于普通“发现”。
The parties exchanged documents during discovery.
双方在证据开示阶段交换了文件。
The lawyer requested more time for discovery.
律师申请延长证据开示时间。
The discovery of a leak changed our schedule.
发现漏水后，我们改变了日程。（普通义。）

02. damages · 关键词 · 回看 00:39:08
损害赔偿金
字幕中出现：damages
法律义通常用复数；damage 常指损害本身，注意区别。
The court awarded damages to the customer.
法院判给客户损害赔偿金。
They are seeking damages for the loss.
他们正就损失请求赔偿。
The storm caused damage, but the claim is for damages.
暴风雨造成了损害，而索赔请求的是赔偿金。

银行调查了可能的欺诈行为。
The company introduced fraud prevention training.
公司开展了防欺诈培训。
She realized the offer was a fraud.
她意识到那个邀约是骗局。

04. solicitation · 关键词 · 回看 00:15:39
招揽；请求；教唆或邀约
字幕中出现：solicitation

The rules restrict direct solicitation of clients.
规则限制直接招揽客户。
The complaint concerned improper solicitation.
投诉涉及不当招揽行为。
They received a solicitation for donations.
他们收到了募捐请求。

05. spoliation · 关键词 · 回看 00:17:41
证据毁损、灭失或隐匿
字幕中出现：spoliation

记录缺失引出了证据毁损问题。
The court considered sanctions for spoliation.
法院考虑对毁损证据进行处罚。
A preservation policy can reduce spoliation risks.
证据保存制度可以降低证据毁损风险。

他否认有意欺诈银行。

12. malfeasance · 关键词 · 回看 00:17:15

13. smoking gun · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:29:49
确凿的关键证据
字幕中出现：smoking gun
比喻直接显示责任或真相的强力证据；不必是实际枪支。
The email appeared to be a smoking gun.
邮件似乎是确凿的关键证据。
They searched for a smoking gun.
他们寻找能一锤定音的证据。
A suspicious payment is not always a smoking gun.
一笔可疑付款未必就是铁证。

14. summary judgment · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:38:24
简易判决；无需完整审判作出的判决
字幕中出现：summary judgment
美国民事程序中通常涉及不存在实质事实争议等条件；不是普通“总结”。
The defendant sought summary judgment.
被告申请简易判决。
The court denied summary judgment.
法院拒绝作出简易判决。
The motion argued that summary judgment was appropriate.
动议主张适合采用简易判决。
