英文梗概（73 words，含剧透）
While helping an elderly client, Jimmy notices suspicious charges at Sandpiper Crossing. He investigates the billing, retrieves shredded documents and discovers a possible pattern of financial exploitation. Chuck becomes absorbed in the legal work and helps develop a substantial case. Their collaboration gives Jimmy hope that they can finally work as equals. As the case grows beyond a solo practice, the need for resources creates a new question about who will control it.

重点词汇：14 项（单词与词组合计）
关键词采用常见美式读法的宽式 IPA；/ɝ/、/ɚ/ 表示带 r 色彩的元音，/i/、/u/ 不另加长音符号。音标对应显示的词条形式。词性列出本页讲解的用法；部分词有其他读法或词性。

01. discovery · 关键词 · 回看 00:43:03
美式 IPA：/dɪˈskʌvɚi/
词性：noun
English definition: Finding something previously unknown; in litigation, the process of obtaining and exchanging case information.
发现；诉讼中的证据开示
字幕中出现：discovery
法律义是审前依法获取对方相关信息的程序，区别于普通“发现”。
The parties exchanged documents during discovery.
双方在证据开示阶段交换了文件。
The lawyer requested more time for discovery.
律师申请延长证据开示时间。
The discovery of a leak changed our schedule.
发现漏水后，我们改变了日程。（普通义。）

02. damages · 关键词 · 回看 00:39:08
美式 IPA：/ˈdæmɪdʒɪz/
词性：plural noun
English definition: Money awarded to compensate for a legally recognized injury or loss.
原形：damage
损害赔偿金
字幕中出现：damages
法律义通常用复数；damage 常指损害本身，注意区别。
The court awarded damages to the customer.
法院判给客户损害赔偿金。
They are seeking damages for the loss.
他们正就损失请求赔偿。
The storm caused damage, but the claim is for damages.
暴风雨造成了损害，而索赔请求的是赔偿金。

03. fraud · 关键词 · 回看 00:14:26
美式 IPA：/frɔd/
词性：noun
English definition: Deliberate deception intended to obtain a benefit or cause another person's loss.
欺诈
字幕中出现：Fraud
法律或商务语境指欺骗行为；a fraud 也可指骗子或冒牌者。
The bank investigated possible fraud.
银行调查了可能的欺诈行为。
The company introduced fraud prevention training.
公司开展了防欺诈培训。
She realized the offer was a fraud.
她意识到那个邀约是骗局。

04. solicitation · 关键词 · 回看 00:15:39
美式 IPA：/səˌlɪsɪˈteɪʃən/
词性：noun
English definition: The act of requesting business, money, or action; in some legal contexts, urging someone to commit an offense.
招揽；请求；教唆或邀约
字幕中出现：solicitation
法律中可指招揽客户或某类犯罪邀约；须看宾语和场景。
The rules restrict direct solicitation of clients.
规则限制直接招揽客户。
The complaint concerned improper solicitation.
投诉涉及不当招揽行为。
They received a solicitation for donations.
他们收到了募捐请求。

05. spoliation · 关键词 · 回看 00:17:41
美式 IPA：/ˌspoʊliˈeɪʃən/
词性：noun
English definition: The destruction, alteration, or improper loss of evidence relevant to a legal dispute.
证据毁损、灭失或隐匿
字幕中出现：spoliation
法律术语 spoliation of evidence；不要误认成普通 spoilage。
The missing records raised a spoliation issue.
记录缺失引出了证据毁损问题。
The court considered sanctions for spoliation.
法院考虑对毁损证据进行处罚。
A preservation policy can reduce spoliation risks.
证据保存制度可以降低证据毁损风险。

06. litigation · 关键词 · 回看 00:17:28
美式 IPA：/ˌlɪtəˈɡeɪʃən/
词性：noun
English definition: The process of pursuing or defending a dispute through the courts.
诉讼；进行诉讼的过程
字幕中出现：litigation
通常不可数；litigation costs 诉讼费用。
The dispute led to years of litigation.
争议导致多年诉讼。
Litigation can be expensive and slow.
诉讼可能昂贵且漫长。
She works in commercial litigation.
她从事商事诉讼工作。

07. defraud · 关键词 · 回看 00:17:28
美式 IPA：/dɪˈfrɔd/
词性：verb
English definition: To obtain money, property, or another benefit from someone through deception.
欺诈以骗取钱财或权利
字幕中出现：defrauding
defraud someone of something；比一般 deceive 更具体。
The scheme defrauded investors of millions.
骗局骗走了投资者数百万资金。
He denied intending to defraud the bank.
他否认有意欺诈银行。
Customers were defrauded through false invoices.
客户因虚假发票而被骗。

08. statute · 关键词 · 回看 00:02:07
美式 IPA：/ˈstætʃut/
词性：noun
English definition: A written law enacted by a legislative body.
成文法；法令
字幕中出现：statute
与 statue 雕像区分；法律通常是 legislature 制定的 statute。
The statute defines the offence.
成文法界定了该罪。
Counsel cited the relevant statute.
律师引用了相关法条。
The claim depends on how the statute is interpreted.
诉求取决于如何解释该法令。

09. precedent · 关键词 · 回看 00:30:03
美式 IPA：/ˈprɛsɪdənt/
词性：noun
English definition: An earlier decision or event used as a guide for later similar situations.
先例；具有参考或约束意义的判例
字幕中出现：precedent
set a precedent 开创先例；与 president 总统区分。
The ruling set a precedent.
裁决开创了先例。
Counsel cited a relevant precedent.
律师引用了相关判例。
There was no clear precedent for the decision.
这个决定没有明确先例。

10. sanction · 关键词 · 回看 00:23:45
美式 IPA：/ˈsæŋkʃən/
词性：noun / verb
English definition: An official penalty or approval; as a verb, to penalize or formally authorize, depending on context.
处罚；正式批准
字幕中出现：sanctioned
含近乎相反的双义：impose sanctions 施加制裁；sanction a decision 批准。
The court sanctioned the lawyer.
法院处罚了律师。
The board refused to sanction the proposal.
董事会拒绝批准提案。
The policy includes sanctions for misconduct.
政策规定了对不当行为的处罚。

11. evidentiary · 关键词 · 回看 00:38:57
美式 IPA：/ˌɛvɪˈdɛnʃiˌɛri/
词性：adjective
English definition: Relating to evidence or the rules governing its use.
证据方面的
字幕中出现：evidentiary
形容词，evidentiary hearing 专门审查证据问题的听证。
The court scheduled an evidentiary hearing.
法院安排了证据听证。
There was an evidentiary gap.
证据存在缺口。
Counsel raised an evidentiary objection.
律师提出了证据方面的异议。

12. malfeasance · 关键词 · 回看 00:17:15
美式 IPA：/ˌmælˈfizəns/
词性：noun
English definition: Unlawful or seriously improper conduct, especially by someone holding a position of responsibility.
违法失职；不法行为
字幕中出现：malfeasance
正式词，尤其用于任职或履职时的违法不当行为。
The audit uncovered financial malfeasance.
审计揭露了财务不法行为。
She denied any malfeasance in office.
她否认任职期间存在违法失职。
The allegation concerned corporate malfeasance.
指控涉及企业不法行为。

13. smoking gun · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:29:49
词性：noun phrase / idiom
English definition: A piece of evidence that strongly and directly reveals wrongdoing.
确凿的关键证据
字幕中出现：smoking gun
比喻直接显示责任或真相的强力证据；不必是实际枪支。
The email appeared to be a smoking gun.
邮件似乎是确凿的关键证据。
They searched for a smoking gun.
他们寻找能一锤定音的证据。
A suspicious payment is not always a smoking gun.
一笔可疑付款未必就是铁证。

14. summary judgment · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:38:24
词性：noun phrase
English definition: A decision made without a trial when no genuine dispute of material fact requires one and the law determines the outcome.
简易判决；无需完整审判作出的判决
字幕中出现：summary judgment
美国民事程序中通常涉及不存在实质事实争议等条件；不是普通“总结”。
The defendant sought summary judgment.
被告申请简易判决。
The court denied summary judgment.
法院拒绝作出简易判决。
The motion argued that summary judgment was appropriate.
动议主张适合采用简易判决。
