01. prosecution · 关键词 · 回看 00:39:50
美式 IPA：/ˌprɑsəˈkjuʃən/
词性：noun
English definition: The pursuit of criminal charges in court; also the lawyers bringing those charges.
控方；起诉；检控
字幕中出现：Prosecution
the prosecution 常指刑事案件中的控方。
The prosecution presented its evidence.
控方出示了证据。
The defense challenged the prosecution's account.
辩方质疑了控方的说法。
The file was sent for prosecution.
案卷被移送起诉。

02. discovery · 关键词 · 回看 00:45:24
美式 IPA：/dɪˈskʌvɚi/
词性：noun
English definition: Finding something previously unknown; in litigation, the process of obtaining and exchanging case information.
发现；诉讼中的证据开示
字幕中出现：discovery
法律义是审前依法获取对方相关信息的程序，区别于普通“发现”。
The parties exchanged documents during discovery.
双方在证据开示阶段交换了文件。
The lawyer requested more time for discovery.
律师申请延长证据开示时间。
The discovery of a leak changed our schedule.
发现漏水后，我们改变了日程。（普通义。）

03. restitution · 关键词 · 回看 00:43:58
美式 IPA：/ˌrɛstəˈtuʃən/
词性：noun
English definition: Returning property or paying compensation to someone who suffered a loss.
返还财物；对损失的补偿
字幕中出现：restitution
法律语境常指向受害者偿还；不同于惩罚性罚款。
The court ordered restitution.
法院命令返还财物或赔偿损失。
He agreed to pay restitution to the victims.
他同意向受害者赔偿。
Restitution did not erase the offence.
赔偿并未抹去违法行为。

04. waive · 关键词 · 回看 00:39:59

English definition: To voluntarily give up a right, requirement, claim, or fee.
自愿放弃权利、要求或费用
字幕中出现：waiving
waive a right/fee；与 wave 挥手区分。
She waived her right to remain silent.
她放弃了保持沉默的权利。
The bank waived the fee.
银行免除了费用。
They refused to waive the requirement.
他们拒绝免除这项要求。

05. statute · 关键词 · 回看 00:40:01

词性：noun
English definition: A written law enacted by a legislative body.
成文法；法令
字幕中出现：statute
与 statue 雕像区分；法律通常是 legislature 制定的 statute。
The statute defines the offence.
成文法界定了该罪。
Counsel cited the relevant statute.
律师引用了相关法条。
The claim depends on how the statute is interpreted.
诉求取决于如何解释该法令。

06. remorse · 关键词 · 回看 00:27:01

他表现出真诚的悔意。
She felt remorse for deceiving her friend.
她因欺骗朋友感到内疚。
The apology contained little remorse.

08. acquit · 关键词 · 回看 00:22:34
美式 IPA：/əˈkwɪt/
词性：verb
English definition: To decide in court that a defendant has not been proved guilty.
宣判无罪
字幕中出现：acquitted

The jury acquitted her of fraud.
陪审团判她诈骗罪不成立。
He was acquitted on all counts.
他的所有指控均被判不成立。
An acquittal ended the criminal trial.
无罪判决结束了刑事审判。

11. statute of limitations · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:40:01

诉讼或追诉时效规定
字幕中出现：statute of limitations

双方对时效规定存在争议。
They filed before the statute of limitations expired.
他们在时效届满前提起诉讼。
Counsel checked the applicable statute of limitations.
律师核查了适用的时效规定。

12. bar hearing · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:45:01
