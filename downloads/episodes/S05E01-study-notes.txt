01. felony · 关键词 · 回看 00:24:12
美式 IPA：/ˈfɛləni/
词性：noun
English definition: A serious category of crime, generally carrying more severe penalties than a misdemeanor.
重罪
字幕中出现：felonies
美国刑事分类用语；具体定义和后果依司法辖区及法律而定。
He was charged with a felony.
他被控犯有重罪。
The form asks about felony convictions.
表格询问是否有重罪定罪记录。
The lawyer explained the difference between a felony and a misdemeanor.
律师解释了重罪和轻罪的区别。

02. retainer · 关键词 · 回看 00:43:56
美式 IPA：/rɪˈteɪnɚ/
词性：noun
English definition: A fee or arrangement securing a professional's services or availability, especially a lawyer's.
聘用律师等专业人士的预付费用；聘用安排
字幕中出现：retainer
具体如何计费取决于协议；不必然指已赚取的费用。
The client paid a retainer before work began.
客户在工作开始前支付了预付聘用费。
Ask what the retainer covers.
问清预付费用覆盖哪些服务。
The firm explained its retainer agreement.
事务所解释了聘用协议。

03. settlement · 关键词 · 回看 00:46:38
美式 IPA：/ˈsɛtəlmənt/
词性：noun
English definition: An agreement resolving a dispute, or the money paid under that agreement.
和解；和解协议或款项
字幕中出现：settlement
诉讼中常指双方协议解决争议；不等于法院判决。
The parties reached a settlement.
双方达成了和解。
They rejected the first settlement offer.
他们拒绝了第一次和解报价。
Is the settlement in writing?
和解协议有书面形式吗？

04. misconduct · 关键词 · 回看 00:45:54

词性：noun
English definition: Behavior that violates rules, duties, or expected standards.
不当行为；违规失职
字幕中出现：misconduct
通常不可数；professional misconduct 职业不当行为。
The panel investigated professional misconduct.
小组调查了职业不当行为。
He was dismissed for serious misconduct.
他因严重违规被解雇。
The complaint alleged prosecutorial misconduct.
投诉指称检方存在不当行为。

05. compensate · 关键词 · 回看 00:44:24
美式 IPA：/ˈkɑmpənˌseɪt/
词性：verb
English definition: To provide payment or another benefit for loss, harm, or work; to make up for a disadvantage.
赔偿；补偿；弥补
字幕中出现：compensated
compensate someone for a loss；compensate for a weakness。
The company compensated her for the damage.
公司赔偿了她的损失。
Experience can compensate for limited resources.
经验可以弥补资源不足。
Nothing could fully compensate him for the lost time.
没有什么能完全补偿他失去的时间。

06. confession · 关键词 · 回看 00:28:04
美式 IPA：/kənˈfɛʃən/
词性：noun
English definition: An admission that one has committed a crime or done something wrong.
供认；坦白
字幕中出现：confession
confession to a crime；不自动意味着供述真实或可采。
The detective recorded the confession.
侦探录下了供词。
Her confession changed the investigation.
她的坦白改变了调查方向。
The defence questioned whether the confession was voluntary.
辩方质疑供述是否出于自愿。

07. legitimate · 关键词 · 回看 00:28:08
美式 IPA：/ləˈdʒɪtəmət/
词性：adjective
English definition: Allowed by law or accepted rules; also genuine or reasonably justified.
合法正当的；真实合理的
字幕中出现：legitimate
legitimate concern 合理担忧；不局限于合法性。
They run a legitimate business.
他们经营合法生意。

那是合理的担忧。

我们核查了文件是否真实有效。

08. discreet · 关键词 · 回看 00:27:11
美式 IPA：/dɪˈskrit/
词性：adjective
English definition: Careful to avoid attracting attention or revealing private information.
谨慎低调的；守口如瓶的
字幕中出现：discreet
勿与 discrete“分离的”混淆；discreet enquiry 低调询问。
We need a discreet investigation.
我们需要低调调查。
She was discreet about the client's affairs.
她对客户的事务守口如瓶。
Make a discreet enquiry before proceeding.
继续之前先谨慎打听一下。

09. sentencing · 关键词 · 回看 00:47:42
美式 IPA：/ˈsɛntənsɪŋ/
词性：noun
English definition: The stage of a criminal case at which a court determines the punishment.
原形：sentence
量刑；宣判刑罚
字幕中出现：sentencing
通常发生在认罪或被判有罪之后；与定罪 conviction 区分。
Sentencing was set for next month.
量刑安排在下个月。
The judge reviewed the sentencing recommendations.
法官审阅了量刑建议。
She spoke at the sentencing hearing.
她在量刑听证中发言。

地方检察官宣布了指控。
She works in the district attorney's office.
她在地方检察官办公室工作。
