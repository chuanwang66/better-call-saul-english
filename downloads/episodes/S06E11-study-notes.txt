英文梗概（80 words，含剧透）
Flashbacks revisit Saul's first encounter with Walt and Jesse and Mike's warning about Walt's risks. In Omaha, Gene contacts Francesca and learns about the collapse of his former world. A call to Kim ends badly, and he turns toward a more predatory series of scams with Jeff and Buddy. They target wealthy men and steal financial information. Buddy refuses to rob a man with cancer, but Gene insists on continuing, revealing how far his need for control has overtaken restraint.

01. privilege · 关键词 · 回看 00:32:43
法律保密特权；特殊权利或荣幸
字幕中出现：privilege
法律中可指受保护、不须披露的沟通；不同于一般 confidentiality。
Counsel raised a claim of privilege.
律师主张适用保密特权。
The conversation may be protected by privilege.
谈话可能受保密特权保护。
Access to the archive is a privilege.
查阅档案是一项特殊待遇。

02. unencumbered · 关键词 · 回看 00:06:25
不受拖累或束缚的；无负担的
字幕中出现：Unencumbered
unencumbered by；资产也可指未设抵押等负担。
She travelled unencumbered by luggage.
她旅行时没有行李拖累。
The property was unencumbered.
房产没有相关权利负担。
They wanted a discussion unencumbered by old disputes.
他们希望讨论不受旧争端束缚。

律师助理整理了案件档案。
She trained as a paralegal.
她接受了律师助理培训。
A paralegal helped prepare the documents.
律师助理协助准备了文件。

05. appraisal · 关键词 · 回看 00:49:03
评估；估价
字幕中出现：appraisal
可指专业估值，也可指对人的表现或情况的判断。
The appraisal valued the house at a higher price.
估价报告给房屋定了更高的价格。
His appraisal of the risks was optimistic.
他对风险的评估偏乐观。
We need an independent appraisal.
我们需要独立评估。

06. under false pretenses · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:10:50
借虚假理由；通过虚假陈述
字幕中出现：under false pretenses
美式 pretenses，英式 pretences；不是一般隐瞒情绪。
He entered the building under false pretenses.
他借虚假理由进入建筑。
They obtained the records under false pretenses.
他们用虚假陈述取得记录。
She accused him of borrowing money under false pretenses.
她指责他以虚假理由借钱。

07. aspersions · 关键词 · 回看 00:02:54
诋毁；恶意影射
字幕中出现：aspersions
通常复数，与 cast 搭配：cast aspersions on。
Her remarks were full of aspersions.
她的话充满了恶意影射。
They rejected the aspersions on their competence.
他们驳斥了对其能力的诋毁。
We need facts rather than aspersions.
我们需要事实，而不是恶意中伤。

08. fencing · 关键词 · 回看 00:49:50
销赃
字幕中出现：fencing
犯罪语境来自 fence：买卖赃物；另有击剑、围栏等无关义。
The crew was fencing stolen jewellery.
团伙正在倒卖被盗珠宝。
Police investigated a fencing operation.
警方调查了一项销赃活动。
He made money fencing stolen electronics.
他通过倒卖被盗电子产品赚钱。
