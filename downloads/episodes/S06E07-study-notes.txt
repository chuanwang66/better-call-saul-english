风骚律师逐集英语学习册 · S06E07 Plan and Execution
英文梗概（76 words，含剧透）
Jimmy and Kim execute their plan against Howard during the Sandpiper mediation. Altered photographs and a drugged Howard make his accusations appear irrational, pushing the case toward settlement. Howard later confronts them at their apartment and describes the damage they have caused. Lalo arrives unexpectedly during the argument. Before Jimmy or Kim can understand or control the situation, he shoots Howard. Their elaborate manipulation collides with violence that has no place in the story they planned.

01. settlement · 关键词 · 回看 00:45:56
和解；和解协议或款项
字幕中出现：settlement
诉讼中常指双方协议解决争议；不等于法院判决。
The parties reached a settlement.
双方达成了和解。
They rejected the first settlement offer.
他们拒绝了第一次和解报价。
Is the settlement in writing?
和解协议有书面形式吗？

02. conspiracy · 关键词 · 回看 00:12:06
共谋；阴谋
字幕中出现：conspiracy
法律语境强调共同约定实施违法行为；也用于非法律的秘密计划。
Prosecutors alleged a conspiracy to commit fraud.
检方指控存在诈骗共谋。
She denied taking part in the conspiracy.
她否认参与阴谋。
The conspiracy involved several companies.
共谋涉及几家公司。

03. sanction · 关键词 · 回看 00:33:17
处罚；正式批准
字幕中出现：sanction
含近乎相反的双义：impose sanctions 施加制裁；sanction a decision 批准。
本集用法：本集作动词表示正式批准或认可决定，不是实施处罚。
The court sanctioned the lawyer.
法院处罚了律师。
The board refused to sanction the proposal.
董事会拒绝批准提案。
The policy includes sanctions for misconduct.
政策规定了对不当行为的处罚。

04. covert · 关键词 · 回看 00:11:59
秘密进行的；隐蔽的
字幕中出现：covert
与 overt 公开的相对；covert operation 秘密行动。
They conducted covert surveillance.
他们进行了秘密监视。
The funds supported a covert operation.
资金支持了一次秘密行动。
His opposition was covert at first.
他的反对起初是暗中的。

05. mediation · 关键词 · 回看 00:33:10
调解
字幕中出现：mediation
由中立第三方协助达成协议；不同于作出裁决的 arbitration。
The parties agreed to mediation.
双方同意接受调解。
Mediation failed to resolve the dispute.
调解未能解决争端。
The agreement emerged from mediation.
协议是在调解中达成的。

06. mediator · 关键词 · 回看 00:20:52
调解员；调解人
字幕中出现：mediator
帮助双方沟通和协商，通常不替双方作决定。
The mediator asked both sides to pause.
调解员请双方暂停。
They appointed an independent mediator.
他们指定了一位独立调解员。
A mediator helped them explore alternatives.
调解员帮助他们探索替代方案。

07. compromise · 关键词 · 回看 00:25:30
妥协；折中；危及
字幕中出现：compromise
名词指折中方案；动词 compromise security 指损害安全。
Both sides accepted a compromise.
双方接受了折中方案。
They refused to compromise on safety.
他们拒绝在安全上妥协。
The leaked password compromised the system.
泄露的密码危及了系统安全。

小说描写了一个有魅力的反社会者。

她不接受随意贴上反社会者标签。
