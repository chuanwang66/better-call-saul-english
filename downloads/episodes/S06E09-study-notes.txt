英文梗概（74 words，含剧透）
Jimmy and Kim try to live with the cover story after Howard's death. At his memorial, Kim reinforces the false account, then recognizes what their partnership has become. She quits the law and leaves Jimmy, admitting that she knew Lalo was alive. Gus preserves his cartel position, while Mike visits Nacho's father and finds that promises of justice cannot erase the loss. 01. consensus · 关键词 · 回看 00:17:35
美式 IPA：/kənˈsɛnsəs/
词性：noun
English definition: General agreement among the members of a group.
共识；一致意见
字幕中出现：consensus
常用 reach a consensus；并不一定表示所有人绝对赞成。
The committee reached a consensus.
委员会达成了共识。
There is no consensus on the cause.
关于原因尚无共识。
The proposal reflected the team's consensus.
提案反映了团队的共同意见。

02. confession · 关键词 · 回看 00:22:48
美式 IPA：/kənˈfɛʃən/
词性：noun
English definition: An admission that one has committed a crime or done something wrong.
供认；坦白
字幕中出现：confession
confession to a crime；不自动意味着供述真实或可采。
The detective recorded the confession.
侦探录下了供词。
Her confession changed the investigation.
她的坦白改变了调查方向。
The defence questioned whether the confession was voluntary.
辩方质疑供述是否出于自愿。

03. class action · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:52:37
词性：noun phrase
English definition: A lawsuit brought on behalf of a group whose members have similar legal claims.
集体诉讼
字幕中出现：class action
由代表成员为具有共同问题的一类人提起的诉讼；资格须依法认定。
The customers filed a class action.
顾客提起了集体诉讼。
The court considered whether to certify the class action.
法院审议是否批准集体诉讼资格。
The notice explains who is included in the class action.
通知解释了哪些人属于集体诉讼成员。

04. pull the plug · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:49:03
词性：verb phrase / idiom
English definition: To end a project, arrangement, or source of support.
终止；停止支持
字幕中出现：pull the plug
pull the plug on a project；也有关闭维持生命设备的字面义。
They pulled the plug on the expansion.
他们终止了扩张计划。
The investor threatened to pull the plug.
投资者威胁要停止支持。
We should not pull the plug too early.
我们不应过早终止。

05. withdraw · 关键词 · 回看 00:41:43
美式 IPA：/wɪðˈdrɔ/
词性：verb
English definition: To remove or take back something; to leave an activity; or to take money from an account.
撤回；退出；取款
字幕中出现：withdraw
withdraw from a case 退出代理；withdraw a claim 撤回诉求。
Counsel requested permission to withdraw.
律师请求获准退出代理。
She withdrew the allegation.
她撤回了指控。
He withdrew cash before travelling.
他旅行前取了现金。

06. downsizing · 关键词 · 回看 00:33:14
美式 IPA：/ˈdaʊnˌsaɪzɪŋ/
词性：noun
English definition: A reduction in an organization's workforce, operations, or size.
原形：downsize
缩减规模；裁员
字幕中出现：downsizing
商务常用委婉表达；动词 downsize。
Downsizing affected the legal department.
裁员波及了法务部门。
The firm announced another round of downsizing.
公司宣布再次缩减规模。
They are downsizing to a smaller office.
他们缩小规模，搬到较小的办公室。

07. oblivious · 关键词 · 回看 00:36:51
美式 IPA：/əˈblɪviəs/
词性：adjective
English definition: Unaware of something happening nearby or affecting oneself.
未察觉的；浑然不知的
字幕中出现：oblivious
oblivious to/of something。
He was oblivious to the tension.
他完全没察觉到紧张气氛。
She seemed oblivious of the risks.
她似乎对风险浑然不知。
They worked on, oblivious to the noise.
他们继续工作，没注意噪音。

08. compelling · 关键词 · 回看 00:22:57
美式 IPA：/kəmˈpɛlɪŋ/
词性：adjective
English definition: Strong enough to persuade someone or hold their attention.
原形：compel
很有说服力的；引人入胜的
字幕中出现：compelling
compelling evidence/argument；不等于已经确定为真。
She presented a compelling argument.
她提出了很有说服力的论证。
The evidence was compelling but incomplete.
证据很有力，但并不完整。
It is a compelling account of ambition.
这是一个引人入胜的野心故事。

09. petition · 关键词 · 回看 00:43:07

They filed a petition with the court.
他们向法院提交了申请。
Residents signed a petition.
居民签署了请愿书。
She petitioned for a review.
她正式申请复核。
