01. diversify · 关键词 · 回看 00:24:31
美式 IPA：/daɪˈvɝsəˌfaɪ/
词性：verb
English definition: To add different activities or investments, often to spread risk.
分散投资；使多样化
字幕中出现：diversify
diversify a portfolio；并不保证没有风险。
They diversified into consulting.
他们把业务拓展到咨询领域。
She diversified her investments.
她分散了投资。
The firm needs to diversify its client base.
公司需要让客户来源更多样。

02. sunk cost · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:22:00
词性：noun phrase
English definition: A cost already incurred that cannot be recovered by a future decision.
沉没成本
字幕中出现：Sunk Cost
已经发生、无法回收的成本；相关谬误是因既有投入而继续错误决策。
The old software licence is a sunk cost.
旧软件许可证费用是沉没成本。
Don't let a sunk cost dictate the next decision.
别让沉没成本支配下一步决定。
They treated the initial payment as a sunk cost.
他们把首付款视为沉没成本。

03. fallacy · 关键词 · 回看 00:22:00
美式 IPA：/ˈfæləsi/
词性：noun
English definition: An error in reasoning or a belief based on faulty reasoning.
错误推理；谬误
字幕中出现：Fallacy
不是单纯的事实错误；sunk-cost fallacy 沉没成本谬误。
That argument rests on a fallacy.
这个论证建立在错误推理上。
She identified a common fallacy.
她指出了一个常见谬误。
The sunk-cost fallacy can distort decisions.
沉没成本谬误可能扭曲决策。

04. dividends · 关键词 · 回看 00:28:25
美式 IPA：/ˈdɪvəˌdɛndz/
词性：plural noun
English definition: Payments of company profits to shareholders; figuratively, benefits produced by an effort.
原形：dividend
股息；红利；回报
字幕中出现：dividends
通常复数；pay dividends 也比喻产生好处。
The company pays dividends quarterly.
公司按季度派发股息。
Patience can pay dividends.
耐心可能带来回报。
They reinvested the dividends.
他们将股息再次投入。

05. inheritance · 关键词 · 回看 00:24:13
美式 IPA：/ɪnˈhɛrətəns/
词性：noun
English definition: Property or money received from someone after their death.
继承的财产；继承
字幕中出现：inheritance
区别于 heritage 文化遗产；receive an inheritance。
She used her inheritance to repay debt.
她用继承的财产还债。
The inheritance caused a family dispute.
遗产引发了家庭纠纷。
He had no expectation of an inheritance.
他没指望继承财产。

06. irrespective · 关键词 · 回看 00:40:36
美式 IPA：/ˌɪrəˈspɛktɪv/
词性：adjective (in irrespective of)
English definition: Used with of to mean without considering or being affected by something.
不考虑；无论
字幕中出现：irrespective
固定结构 irrespective of，相当于 regardless of，较正式。
The rule applies irrespective of age.
规则不论年龄都适用。
They proceeded irrespective of the objections.
他们不顾反对继续推进。
Payment is due irrespective of the outcome.
无论结果如何都应付款。
