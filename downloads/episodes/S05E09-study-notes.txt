01. bail · 关键词 · 回看 00:16:27
美式 IPA：/beɪl/
词性：noun / verb
English definition: Conditional release before trial, sometimes secured by money; to secure someone's release in this way.
保释；保释金
字幕中出现：bail
常用 on bail 或 post bail；保释不表示无罪。
The defendant was released on bail.
被告获准保释。
The family helped him post bail.
家人帮助他缴纳保释金。
The judge considered the request for bail.
法官审议了保释申请。

02. for the record · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:56:20
词性：adverb phrase
English definition: Used to make one's position explicit so that it cannot easily be misunderstood later.
为正式记录说明；把话说清楚
字幕中出现：for the record
可用于法庭或会议，也用于口语纠正他人印象。
本集用法：本集是强调立场、把话说清楚的口语用法，不是正式制作庭审记录。
For the record, I voted against the change.
说清楚，我投了反对这个改动的票。
Please state your name for the record.
请报上姓名以供记录。
For the record, nobody asked me to leave.
我要说明，并没有人要求我离开。

03. plea deal · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:23:47
词性：noun phrase
English definition: An agreement exchanging a guilty plea for specified concessions concerning charges or sentencing.
认罪协商协议
字幕中出现：plea deal
刑事语境，通常以认罪换取控方某些让步；具体内容须看协议及法院程序。
The lawyers discussed a possible plea deal.
律师们讨论了可能的认罪协商协议。
The defendant accepted the plea deal.
被告接受了认罪协商协议。
What does the proposed plea deal include?
拟议的认罪协商协议包含什么？

04. pro bono · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:28:30
词性：adjective / adverb phrase
English definition: Provided without a fee for the public good, especially legal services.
无偿提供的专业服务
字幕中出现：pro bono
常指律师为公益无偿服务；并非所有免费服务都属于此义。
The lawyer accepted the case pro bono.
律师无偿承办了此案。
Our firm supports pro bono work.
我们事务所支持公益法律服务。
She spends one day a month on pro bono cases.
她每月用一天处理公益案件。

05. asset · 关键词 · 回看 00:18:04
美式 IPA：/ˈæsɛt/
词性：noun
English definition: A person or resource considered useful for achieving a particular goal.
资产；有价值的人或优势
字幕中出现：asset
可数名词；对人使用时常强调其工具价值，语气可能冷峻。
本集用法：本集用来指有利用价值的人或资源，体现说话者冷静的工具化视角。
Cash is the firm's most liquid asset.
现金是公司流动性最强的资产。
Her local knowledge is a valuable asset.
她对当地的了解是一项宝贵优势。
They treated the informant as an asset.
他们把线人视为可利用的资源。

06. continuance · 关键词 · 回看 00:23:51
美式 IPA：/kənˈtɪnjuəns/
词性：noun
English definition: A court-authorized postponement of a hearing or other proceeding.
庭审或诉讼程序的延期
字幕中出现：continuance
美国法律名词；request/grant a continuance。
The defence requested a continuance.
辩方申请延期。
The judge granted a short continuance.
法官批准短期延期。
A continuance allowed time to review the evidence.
延期提供了审查证据的时间。

07. unimpeachable · 关键词 · 回看 00:17:26
美式 IPA：/ˌʌnɪmˈpitʃəbəl/
词性：adjective
English definition: So reliable or honorable that there is little basis for questioning it.
无可指摘的；极其可靠的
字幕中出现：unimpeachable
常形容品格或可信度；与政治 impeach 的具体程序区分。
The witness had unimpeachable credentials.
证人的资历无可指摘。
We need an unimpeachable source.
我们需要一个极可靠的信息来源。
Her integrity was considered unimpeachable.
她的正直被认为无可挑剔。

08. cut and dried · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:35:13
词性：adjective phrase / idiom
English definition: Already settled or so clear that little debate is needed.
清楚明确、已经定好的
字幕中出现：Cut and dried
通常作表语；强调没有太多可争论的余地。
The decision wasn't as cut and dried as it seemed.
这个决定并不像看起来那么简单明确。
For him, the issue was cut and dried.
对他来说，这个问题没有争议。
Few ethical questions are completely cut and dried.
很少有伦理问题完全一清二楚。

09. rigmarole · 关键词 · 回看 00:50:20

我们经历了惯常的繁琐手续。
I can't face another round of rigmarole.
我不想再经历一轮繁文缛节。
The application involved endless rigmarole.

10. debacle · 关键词 · 回看 00:32:50
美式 IPA：/dɪˈbɑkəl/
