01. contingent · 关键词 · 回看 00:42:06
美式 IPA：/kənˈtɪndʒənt/
词性：adjective / noun
English definition: Dependent on a condition; as a noun, a group representing a larger organization or community.
取决于条件的；代表团
字幕中出现：contingent
形容词 contingent on/upon；名词 a contingent 指一支队伍或代表团。
The offer is contingent on approval.
这项要约以获批为条件。
Payment is contingent upon completion.
付款取决于是否完成。
A small contingent attended the hearing.
一小队代表参加了听证。

02. under duress · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:02:54
词性：adverb phrase
English definition: While being forced or pressured by threats rather than acting freely.

他说陈述是在胁迫下作出的。
