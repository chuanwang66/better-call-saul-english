01. discovery · 关键词 · 回看 00:01:29
美式 IPA：/dɪˈskʌvɚi/
词性：noun
English definition: Finding something previously unknown; in litigation, the process of obtaining and exchanging case information.
发现；诉讼中的证据开示
字幕中出现：discovery
法律义是审前依法获取对方相关信息的程序，区别于普通“发现”。
The parties exchanged documents during discovery.
双方在证据开示阶段交换了文件。
The lawyer requested more time for discovery.
律师申请延长证据开示时间。
The discovery of a leak changed our schedule.
发现漏水后，我们改变了日程。（普通义。）

02. hearsay · 关键词 · 回看 00:19:07
美式 IPA：/ˈhɪrˌseɪ/
词性：noun
English definition: Information reported secondhand; in evidence law, certain out-of-court statements offered to prove what they assert.
传闻；传闻证据
字幕中出现：hearsay
日常义是听来的信息；法律义和可采性有具体规则及例外。
That story is hearsay, not something I witnessed.
那只是传闻，不是我亲眼所见。
The lawyer objected on hearsay grounds.
律师以传闻证据为由提出异议。
The court considered whether the statement was hearsay.
法院审议该陈述是否属于传闻证据。

03. precedent · 关键词 · 回看 00:06:17
美式 IPA：/ˈprɛsɪdənt/
词性：noun
English definition: An earlier decision or event used as a guide for later similar situations.
先例；具有参考或约束意义的判例
字幕中出现：precedent
set a precedent 开创先例；与 president 总统区分。
The ruling set a precedent.
裁决开创了先例。
Counsel cited a relevant precedent.
律师引用了相关判例。
There was no clear precedent for the decision.
这个决定没有明确先例。

04. public defender · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:20:08
词性：noun phrase
English definition: A publicly funded lawyer who represents eligible defendants unable to afford counsel.
公设辩护律师
字幕中出现：public defender
美国语境常指为符合条件、无力聘律师的被告提供辩护的律师。
A public defender represented the defendant.
一名公设辩护律师代表被告。
She works as a public defender.
她担任公设辩护律师。
The public defender asked for more time to prepare.
公设辩护律师申请更多准备时间。

05. pro bono · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:20:04
词性：adjective / adverb phrase
English definition: Provided without a fee for the public good, especially legal services.
无偿提供的专业服务
字幕中出现：pro bono
常指律师为公益无偿服务；并非所有免费服务都属于此义。
The lawyer accepted the case pro bono.
律师无偿承办了此案。
Our firm supports pro bono work.
我们事务所支持公益法律服务。
She spends one day a month on pro bono cases.
她每月用一天处理公益案件。

06. executor · 关键词 · 回看 00:15:30
美式 IPA：/ɪɡˈzɛkjətɚ/
词性：noun
English definition: A person appointed to administer an estate according to a will.
遗嘱执行人
字幕中出现：executor
负责执行遗嘱和管理遗产事务；与 executioner 行刑人不同。
She appointed her brother as executor.
她指定哥哥为遗嘱执行人。
The executor contacted the beneficiaries.
遗嘱执行人联系了受益人。
He reviewed the executor's accounts.
他审查了遗嘱执行人的账目。

07. perimeter · 关键词 · 回看 00:13:27
美式 IPA：/pəˈrɪmətɚ/
词性：noun
English definition: The outer boundary of an area or a line secured around it.
周界；外围防线
字幕中出现：perimeter
secure/hold the perimeter 常用于保卫现场。
Officers secured the perimeter.
警员封锁了外围。
Cameras covered the warehouse perimeter.
摄像头覆盖了仓库周界。
They checked the perimeter for movement.
他们检查外围是否有动静。

08. asset · 关键词 · 回看 00:02:03
美式 IPA：/ˈæsɛt/
词性：noun
English definition: An item of property or another economic resource with financial value.
资产；有价值的人或优势
字幕中出现：assets
可数名词；对人使用时常强调其工具价值，语气可能冷峻。
本集用法：本集指需要追查的财产或金融资产。
Cash is the firm's most liquid asset.
现金是公司流动性最强的资产。
Her local knowledge is a valuable asset.
她对当地的了解是一项宝贵优势。
They treated the informant as an asset.
他们把线人视为可利用的资源。
