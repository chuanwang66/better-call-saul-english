风骚律师逐集英语学习册 · S01E09 Pimento
英文梗概（75 words，含剧透）
The Sandpiper case is taken to HHM, but the firm will not hire Jimmy. He initially blames Howard, then discovers that Chuck has been blocking his career. Chuck admits that he does not trust Jimmy with the responsibilities of a lawyer. The revelation breaks their apparent partnership. In a parallel story, Mike protects a nervous pharmaceutical employee during a criminal transaction, demonstrating his practical competence and his ability to distinguish professional discipline from empty intimidation.

重点词汇：8 项（单词与词组合计）

01. settlement · 关键词 · 回看 00:22:23
和解；和解协议或款项
字幕中出现：settlement
诉讼中常指双方协议解决争议；不等于法院判决。
The parties reached a settlement.
双方达成了和解。
They rejected the first settlement offer.
他们拒绝了第一次和解报价。
Is the settlement in writing?
和解协议有书面形式吗？

02. fraud · 关键词 · 回看 00:21:36
欺诈
字幕中出现：Fraud
法律或商务语境指欺骗行为；a fraud 也可指骗子或冒牌者。
The bank investigated possible fraud.
银行调查了可能的欺诈行为。
The company introduced fraud prevention training.
公司开展了防欺诈培训。
She realized the offer was a fraud.
她意识到那个邀约是骗局。

03. racketeering · 关键词 · 回看 00:21:38
有组织的非法牟利活动
字幕中出现：racketeering
美国法律中常与 RICO 相关；不是所有诈骗都自动属于此类。
The indictment included racketeering charges.
起诉书包含有组织犯罪相关指控。
Investigators examined alleged racketeering activity.
调查人员审查了涉嫌有组织非法牟利的活动。
The lecture explained civil racketeering claims.
讲座解释了相关民事索赔。

04. deposition · 关键词 · 回看 00:06:04
庭外宣誓证言；取证问询
字幕中出现：deposition
美国诉讼术语，通常在庭前进行并记录，不等于普通访谈。
Her deposition lasted four hours.
她的庭外宣誓问询持续了四小时。
Counsel reviewed the deposition transcript.
律师审阅了证言笔录。
He contradicted his earlier deposition.
他的说法与先前的宣誓证言矛盾。

05. precedent · 关键词 · 回看 00:12:49
先例；具有参考或约束意义的判例
字幕中出现：precedent
set a precedent 开创先例；与 president 总统区分。
The ruling set a precedent.
裁决开创了先例。
Counsel cited a relevant precedent.
律师引用了相关判例。
There was no clear precedent for the decision.
这个决定没有明确先例。

06. compensate · 关键词 · 回看 00:25:28
赔偿；补偿；弥补
字幕中出现：compensated
compensate someone for a loss；compensate for a weakness。
The company compensated her for the damage.
公司赔偿了她的损失。
Experience can compensate for limited resources.
经验可以弥补资源不足。
Nothing could fully compensate him for the lost time.
没有什么能完全补偿他失去的时间。

07. class action · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:21:25
集体诉讼
字幕中出现：class action
由代表成员为具有共同问题的一类人提起的诉讼；资格须依法认定。
The customers filed a class action.
顾客提起了集体诉讼。
The court considered whether to certify the class action.
法院审议是否批准集体诉讼资格。
The notice explains who is included in the class action.
通知解释了哪些人属于集体诉讼成员。

08. restraining order · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:01:34
限制令；禁止特定行为的法院命令
字幕中出现：restraining order
范围依命令内容；不等于警方的普通口头警告。
She applied for a restraining order.
她申请了限制令。
The order prohibited contact.
命令禁止接触。
He was accused of violating a restraining order.
他被指违反限制令。
