英文梗概（71 words，含剧透）
Jimmy wants to leave Davis & Main without losing his bonus, so he makes himself increasingly difficult to keep. His deliberately disruptive behaviour finally gets him fired. He proposes starting a practice with Kim, who prefers separate businesses sharing an office and expenses. The arrangement offers closeness without complete professional dependence. Mike changes his statement about Tuco while planning his own response to Hector. Both men seek independence through carefully calculated compromises.

01. settlement · 关键词 · 回看 00:34:41
美式 IPA：/ˈsɛtəlmənt/
词性：noun
English definition: An agreement resolving a dispute, or the money paid under that agreement.
和解；和解协议或款项
字幕中出现：settlement
诉讼中常指双方协议解决争议；不等于法院判决。
The parties reached a settlement.
双方达成了和解。
They rejected the first settlement offer.
他们拒绝了第一次和解报价。
Is the settlement in writing?
和解协议有书面形式吗？

02. litigation · 关键词 · 回看 00:34:34
美式 IPA：/ˌlɪtəˈɡeɪʃən/
词性：noun
English definition: The process of pursuing or defending a dispute through the courts.
诉讼；进行诉讼的过程
字幕中出现：litigation
通常不可数；litigation costs 诉讼费用。
The dispute led to years of litigation.
争议导致多年诉讼。
Litigation can be expensive and slow.
诉讼可能昂贵且漫长。
She works in commercial litigation.
她从事商事诉讼工作。

03. forensic · 关键词 · 回看 00:06:59
美式 IPA：/fəˈrɛnsɪk/
词性：adjective
English definition: Relating to scientific investigation used to answer legal questions.
法证的；用于司法鉴定的
字幕中出现：forensics
forensic accountant 法务会计；不局限于尸体或刑侦。
A forensic accountant traced the payments.
法务会计追查了款项。
The team examined forensic evidence.
团队审查了法证证据。
Forensic analysis contradicted the initial report.
法证分析与初步报告矛盾。

04. hindsight · 关键词 · 回看 00:06:07
美式 IPA：/ˈhaɪndˌsaɪt/
词性：noun
English definition: Understanding of an event gained after its outcome is known.
事后认识；后见之明
字幕中出现：hindsight
in hindsight 回头来看；常提醒当时无法知道结果。
In hindsight, the warning was clear.
事后看来，警告很明确。
Hindsight made the decision look easy.
事后认识使这个决定看起来很简单。
We should not judge only with hindsight.
我们不应仅凭事后认识来评判。

05. resignation · 关键词 · 回看 00:10:46
美式 IPA：/ˌrɛzɪɡˈneɪʃən/
词性：noun
English definition: The act of leaving a job or position; also acceptance of an unwanted situation.
辞职；无奈接受
字幕中出现：resignation
注意双义：submit a resignation 与 a sigh of resignation。
She submitted her resignation.
她提交了辞呈。
He accepted the result with resignation.
他无奈地接受了结果。
The board requested his resignation.
董事会要求他辞职。

06. grifter · 关键词 · 回看 00:02:23
美式 IPA：/ˈɡrɪftɚ/
词性：noun
English definition: A person who makes money by manipulating or deceiving others.
职业骗子
字幕中出现：grifter
美式口语；强调靠小骗局或欺诈谋生的人。
The supposed investor was a grifter.
所谓的投资者其实是骗子。
A grifter exploited their generosity.
一个骗子利用了他们的慷慨。
The story follows two ageing grifters.
故事讲述两个年迈的职业骗子。

07. malfeasance · 关键词 · 回看 00:20:37
美式 IPA：/ˌmælˈfizəns/
词性：noun
English definition: Unlawful or seriously improper conduct, especially by someone holding a position of responsibility.
违法失职；不法行为
字幕中出现：malfeasance
正式词，尤其用于任职或履职时的违法不当行为。
The audit uncovered financial malfeasance.
审计揭露了财务不法行为。
She denied any malfeasance in office.
她否认任职期间存在违法失职。
The allegation concerned corporate malfeasance.
指控涉及企业不法行为。

08. conflict of interest · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:08:15
词性：noun phrase
English definition: A situation in which competing interests may interfere with impartial judgment or duties.
利益冲突
字幕中出现：conflict of interest
指个人或其他利益可能影响应履行的职责；不只是意见不一致。
Please disclose any conflict of interest.
请披露任何利益冲突。
She stepped aside because of a conflict of interest.
她因利益冲突而退出。
Could accepting this gift create a conflict of interest?
收这份礼物会产生利益冲突吗？

09. a clean slate · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:23:57
词性：noun phrase / idiom
English definition: A fresh start without previous mistakes or obligations counting against someone.
不受旧问题拖累的新起点
字幕中出现：a clean slate
start with a clean slate；强调重新开始，不保证过去的法律责任被免除。
The new team deserves a clean slate.
新团队应当获得重新开始的机会。
We settled our debts and started with a clean slate.
我们还清债务，重新起步。
Moving away did not give him a clean slate.
搬走并没有让他摆脱过去。
