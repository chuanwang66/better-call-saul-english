风骚律师逐集英语学习册 · S01E05 Alpine Shepherd Boy
英文梗概（74 words，含剧透）
Jimmy's publicity brings a series of unsuitable clients, from an eccentric would-be sovereign to an inventor with an unfortunate product. An elderly client's will points him toward elder law, where patience and personal attention may provide a viable practice. Chuck is taken to hospital after an encounter outside his home. Jimmy resists treating his brother as incapable of making decisions. The episode links Jimmy's growing business ambitions with his protective, complicated loyalty to Chuck.

重点词汇：7 项（单词与词组合计）

01. retainer · 关键词 · 回看 00:08:26
聘用律师等专业人士的预付费用；聘用安排
字幕中出现：retainer
具体如何计费取决于协议；不必然指已赚取的费用。
The client paid a retainer before work began.
客户在工作开始前支付了预付聘用费。
Ask what the retainer covers.
问清预付费用覆盖哪些服务。
The firm explained its retainer agreement.
事务所解释了聘用协议。

02. sovereign · 关键词 · 回看 00:07:18
享有主权的；最高统治者
字幕中出现：sovereign
sovereign state 主权国家；剧中也出现个人自称主权的荒诞主张。
The treaty recognized a sovereign state.
条约承认了一个主权国家。
Parliament was described as sovereign.
议会被描述为拥有最高权力。
The claimant called himself a sovereign.
申请人自称独立统治者。

03. incoherent · 关键词 · 回看 00:22:13
语无伦次的；缺乏逻辑的
字幕中出现：incoherent
可形容 speech、argument、policy。
His explanation was incoherent.
他的解释语无伦次。
The report contained an incoherent argument.
报告中的论证缺乏逻辑。
She sounded exhausted and incoherent.
她听起来疲惫不堪，表达混乱。

04. delusion · 关键词 · 回看 00:25:17
妄想；错误而顽固的信念
字幕中出现：delusion
临床语境与日常夸张用法不同；不要随意用来诊断他人。
He was under the delusion that nobody could replace him.
他误以为没人能取代他。
The novel explores ambition and delusion.
小说探讨野心与妄想。
The report described a persistent delusion.
报告描述了一个持续存在的妄想。

05. amendment · 关键词 · 回看 00:03:27
修正案；修订
字幕中出现：Amendment
法律文件的修改；美国 Constitution 的 Amendment 指宪法修正案。
The amendment changed the payment deadline.
修订条款改变了付款期限。
They proposed an amendment to the bill.
他们提议修改法案。
The Fourth Amendment concerns searches and seizures.
第四修正案涉及搜查与扣押。

06. coercive · 关键词 · 回看 00:03:09
强迫性的；带胁迫性质的
字幕中出现：coercive
形容词；coerce 是动词，coercion 是名词。
They criticized the coercive tactics.
他们批评了这些胁迫手段。
The agreement was reached under coercive conditions.
协议是在胁迫条件下达成的。
A coercive threat can undermine consent.
胁迫性威胁可能损害同意的有效性。

07. on retainer · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:08:26
以预付顾问费方式受聘
字幕中出现：on retainer
常用于律师或专业顾问，强调持续可提供服务的安排。
The company keeps a lawyer on retainer.
公司长期付费聘用一位律师。
She works on retainer for several clients.
她为几位客户提供长期顾问服务。
Do we have an investigator on retainer?
我们有长期聘用的调查员吗？
