英文梗概（79 words，含剧透）
Jimmy arranges the theft of a valuable figurine from the copier office, treating it as an easy way to make money. Nacho takes part in a staged attack designed to explain Arturo's death and support Gus's deception. Kim observes court proceedings and becomes interested in people whose cases receive little attention. Gus considers the technical possibilities of a future methamphetamine operation. Chuck's letter to Jimmy produces a restrained response from him and a much more emotional one from Kim.

重点词汇：8 项（单词与词组合计）

01. vulnerable · 关键词 · 回看 00:13:37
易受伤害、攻击或利用的
字幕中出现：vulnerable
vulnerable to；法律、业务和人际中都常见。
The system is vulnerable to attack.
系统容易受到攻击。
The scheme targeted vulnerable clients.
骗局针对容易受害的客户。
She felt vulnerable after the disclosure.
事情披露后，她觉得自己处境脆弱。

02. cut a deal · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:32:29
达成交易或协议
字幕中出现：Cut a deal
非正式，常含协商交换条件的意思；不必然违法。
We cut a deal with the supplier.
我们与供应商谈成了协议。
They hope to cut a deal before Friday.
他们希望周五前谈成协议。
Can we cut a deal that works for both sides?
我们能达成双方都满意的协议吗？

03. due diligence · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:13:11
尽职调查；应有的审慎核查
字幕中出现：due diligence
商务常指交易前查验信息；法律中也可强调合理审慎。
We completed due diligence before buying the business.
收购企业前我们完成了尽职调查。
The investor requested a due diligence report.
投资者要求一份尽职调查报告。
Don't skip due diligence just because the price is low.
别因为价格低就跳过尽职调查。

04. the tip of the iceberg · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:15:21
冰山一角
字幕中出现：the tip of the iceberg
可见问题只是更大隐藏问题的一小部分。
The missing invoice was the tip of the iceberg.
丢失的发票只是冰山一角。
These complaints may be the tip of the iceberg.
这些投诉可能只是冰山一角。
She suspected the error was the tip of the iceberg.
她怀疑这个错误只是更大问题的表面。

05. security-conscious · 关键词 · 回看 00:07:20
有安全防范意识的
字幕中出现：security-conscious
conscious 与名词组合形成“有某方面意识的”形容词。
The firm is highly security-conscious.
公司很重视安全防范。
Security-conscious staff reported the stranger.
有防范意识的员工报告了陌生人。
They became more security-conscious after the theft.
失窃后他们提高了安全意识。

06. swagger · 关键词 · 回看 00:06:49
大摇大摆的姿态；自信甚至傲慢的气派
字幕中出现：swagger
可作名词或动词，常带表演感。
He entered with a confident swagger.
他自信十足地大步走进来。
The character's swagger hides his fear.
角色的傲慢气派掩盖了恐惧。
She swaggered into the office.
她大摇大摆地走进办公室。

07. paralegal · 关键词 · 回看 00:13:55
律师助理；法律辅助人员
字幕中出现：paralegal
不等于有执业资格的律师；职能依地区和机构。
The paralegal organized the case files.
律师助理整理了案件档案。
She trained as a paralegal.
她接受了律师助理培训。
A paralegal helped prepare the documents.
律师助理协助准备了文件。

08. cast aspersions · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:35:08
诋毁；影射他人有问题
字幕中出现：cast aspersions
cast aspersions on someone/something；较正式，常用于否认自己在攻击对方。
He cast aspersions on her integrity.
他对她的正直作了恶意影射。
I don't mean to cast aspersions on the team.
我不是要诋毁这个团队。
The article cast aspersions without offering evidence.
文章没有给出证据就进行影射。
