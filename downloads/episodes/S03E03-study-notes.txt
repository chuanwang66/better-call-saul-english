01. misdemeanor · 关键词 · 回看 00:16:44
美式 IPA：/ˌmɪsdəˈminɚ/
词性：noun
English definition: A criminal offense in a less serious category than a felony.
轻罪
字幕中出现：misdemeanor
美国法律用语；具体分类取决于适用法律，不能只按日常严重程度判断。
The charge was classified as a misdemeanor.
该项指控被归类为轻罪。
She asked whether the offense was a misdemeanor.
她询问该违法行为是否属于轻罪。
The report distinguishes misdemeanors from felonies.
报告区分了轻罪与重罪。

02. felony · 关键词 · 回看 00:16:38
美式 IPA：/ˈfɛləni/
词性：noun
English definition: A serious category of crime, generally carrying more severe penalties than a misdemeanor.
重罪
字幕中出现：felony
美国刑事分类用语；具体定义和后果依司法辖区及法律而定。
He was charged with a felony.
他被控犯有重罪。
The form asks about felony convictions.
表格询问是否有重罪定罪记录。
The lawyer explained the difference between a felony and a misdemeanor.
律师解释了重罪和轻罪的区别。

03. defendant · 关键词 · 回看 00:17:17

被告
字幕中出现：defendant

The defendant entered the courtroom.
被告走进法庭。

The prosecution presented its evidence.

The file was sent for prosecution.
案卷被移送起诉。

05. assault · 关键词 · 回看 00:16:44
美式 IPA：/əˈsɔlt/
词性：noun / verb
English definition: A physical attack or, in some legal uses, conduct that creates fear of imminent harm; to attack.
袭击；人身攻击
字幕中出现：assault

The police investigated the alleged assault.
警方调查了涉嫌袭击事件。
The witness described an assault near the station.
证人描述了车站附近的一起袭击。
The lawyer reviewed the assault charge.
律师审查了袭击指控。

06. disbarment · 关键词 · 回看 00:43:13
美式 IPA：/dɪsˈbɑrmənt/
词性：noun
English definition: The removal of a lawyer's authorization to practice law.
取消律师执业资格
字幕中出现：disbarment
比暂时 suspension 更严重；动词 disbar。
The misconduct could lead to disbarment.
不当行为可能导致吊销律师资格。
He faced disbarment after falsifying records.
他因伪造记录面临取消执业资格。
Disbarment ended her legal practice.
取消资格终结了她的律师执业。

07. suspension · 关键词 · 回看 00:43:07

词性：noun

He returned to practice after the suspension.

11. sunk cost · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:44:20
词性：noun phrase
English definition: A cost already incurred that cannot be recovered by a future decision.
沉没成本
字幕中出现：sunk costs
已经发生、无法回收的成本；相关谬误是因既有投入而继续错误决策。
The old software licence is a sunk cost.
旧软件许可证费用是沉没成本。
Don't let a sunk cost dictate the next decision.
别让沉没成本支配下一步决定。
They treated the initial payment as a sunk cost.
他们把首付款视为沉没成本。

12. breaking and entering · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:16:38
词性：noun phrase
English definition: Unlawfully entering a building, traditionally involving some form of force to gain entry.
破门或非法进入建筑的行为
字幕中出现：breaking and entering
法律定性依地区及具体事实；不是任何“进入”都构成该罪。
Police found evidence of breaking and entering.
警方发现了非法闯入的证据。
The charge involved breaking and entering.
指控涉及非法闯入建筑。
An unlocked door does not automatically settle a breaking-and-entering case.
门没锁并不能自动决定非法闯入案的结论。
