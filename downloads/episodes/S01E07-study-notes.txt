英文梗概（75 words，含剧透）
The Kettlemans reject a plea agreement and abandon Kim for Jimmy, threatening Kim's position at HHM. Jimmy discovers that their expectations are impossible to satisfy honestly. He recruits Mike to recover the stolen cash and ensures that it reaches the authorities, including the money Jimmy had accepted. Without the cash as leverage, the family must return to Kim and face the deal. Jimmy protects Kim's work but loses the money and prospects he briefly imagined.

01. embezzlement · 关键词 · 回看 00:41:37
美式 IPA：/ɪmˈbɛzəlmənt/
词性：noun
English definition: The dishonest taking of money or property entrusted to one's care.
侵占；挪用受托管理的财产
字幕中出现：embezzlement
常见于雇员或管理者挪用经手资金的语境；区别于泛指 theft。
The accountant was accused of embezzlement.
会计被指控侵占受托财产。
The audit uncovered signs of embezzlement.
审计发现了挪用资金的迹象。
The report examined an embezzlement case.
报告研究了一起侵占财产案件。

02. retainer · 关键词 · 回看 00:23:23
美式 IPA：/rɪˈteɪnɚ/
词性：noun
English definition: A fee or arrangement securing a professional's services or availability, especially a lawyer's.
聘用律师等专业人士的预付费用；聘用安排
字幕中出现：retainer
具体如何计费取决于协议；不必然指已赚取的费用。
The client paid a retainer before work began.
客户在工作开始前支付了预付聘用费。
Ask what the retainer covers.
问清预付费用覆盖哪些服务。
The firm explained its retainer agreement.
事务所解释了聘用协议。

字幕中出现：prosecution

The prosecution presented its evidence.
控方出示了证据。
The defense challenged the prosecution's account.

案卷被移送起诉。

04. bribe · 关键词 · 回看 00:41:21
美式 IPA：/braɪb/
词性：noun / verb
English definition: Something offered to improperly influence a decision; to offer such an inducement.
贿赂；贿赂物；行贿
字幕中出现：bribe

The official refused the bribe.
那名官员拒绝了贿赂。
The employee was accused of accepting a bribe.
该员工被指控收受贿赂。
Nobody should have to bribe someone to receive a public service.
任何人都不应该靠行贿才能获得公共服务。

05. immunity · 关键词 · 回看 00:08:50

字幕中出现：immunity

The agreement did not grant blanket immunity.

09. on the hook · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:41:35

10. plead guilty · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:18:24
