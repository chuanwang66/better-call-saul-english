风骚律师逐集英语学习册 · S06E04 Hit and Run
英文梗概（75 words，含剧透）
Jimmy disguises himself as Howard and stages a public confrontation with Wendy using Howard's car. Kim ensures that Cliff Main sees the scene while discussing an ambitious legal-aid project. She notices surveillance and confronts the men following her. Mike explains that Lalo is alive and that she and Jimmy are being watched for their protection. Saul's association with the Salamancas attracts new clients, encouraging him to consider the office that will define his later practice.

重点词汇：10 项（单词与词组合计）

01. retainer · 关键词 · 回看 00:41:28
聘用律师等专业人士的预付费用；聘用安排
字幕中出现：retainers
具体如何计费取决于协议；不必然指已赚取的费用。
The client paid a retainer before work began.
客户在工作开始前支付了预付聘用费。
Ask what the retainer covers.
问清预付费用覆盖哪些服务。
The firm explained its retainer agreement.
事务所解释了聘用协议。

02. bail · 关键词 · 回看 00:42:29
保释；保释金
字幕中出现：bail
常用 on bail 或 post bail；保释不表示无罪。
The defendant was released on bail.
被告获准保释。
The family helped him post bail.
家人帮助他缴纳保释金。
The judge considered the request for bail.
法官审议了保释申请。

03. preliminary · 关键词 · 回看 00:21:30
初步的；正式程序之前的
字幕中出现：preliminary
preliminary hearing 初步听证；不等于最终结果。
The preliminary findings were inconclusive.
初步结果尚无定论。
A preliminary hearing was scheduled.
初步听证已经安排。
They held preliminary discussions.
他们进行了初步讨论。

04. deadlocked · 关键词 · 回看 00:05:30
陷入僵局的
字幕中出现：deadlocked
双方无法取得进展；deadlock 也可作名词。
The negotiations are deadlocked.
谈判陷入了僵局。
The jury remained deadlocked.
陪审团仍无法达成一致。
They were deadlocked over the payment terms.
他们在付款条件上僵持不下。

05. narcissist · 关键词 · 回看 00:17:35
自恋者
字幕中出现：Narcissists
剧中常是尖刻评价；日常标签不等于临床诊断。
The narrator calls his rival a narcissist.
叙述者称对手是个自恋者。
She disliked being labelled a narcissist.
她不喜欢被贴上自恋者的标签。
The fictional narcissist demands constant admiration.
这个虚构的自恋者不断要求别人赞美。

06. status quo · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:04:45
现状
字幕中出现：Status quo
常见 maintain/challenge the status quo；拉丁语借词。
They wanted to maintain the status quo.
他们希望维持现状。
The reform challenged the status quo.
改革挑战了现状。
The status quo no longer worked for her.
现状对她已经行不通了。

07. systemic · 关键词 · 回看 00:11:15
系统性的；影响整个体系的
字幕中出现：systemic
与 systematic“有系统、按部就班的”区分。
The report identified systemic problems.
报告发现了系统性问题。
One appointment cannot fix systemic inequality.
一次任命无法解决系统性不平等。
They called for systemic reform.
他们呼吁系统性改革。

08. by the skin of my teeth · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:17:31
极其勉强地；险些失败
字幕中出现：by the skin of my teeth
人称可变化；强调差一点就没成功或没逃脱。
I caught the train by the skin of my teeth.
我差一点就没赶上火车。
They escaped by the skin of their teeth.
他们险之又险地逃脱了。
She passed the exam by the skin of her teeth.
她勉强通过了考试。

09. made of sterner stuff · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:33:33
更坚强、更有胆识
字幕中出现：made of sterner stuff
比较意味的习语；sterner 是 stern 的比较级。
She is made of sterner stuff than they think.
她比他们想象的更坚强。
You need to be made of sterner stuff for this job.
做这项工作需要更坚韧。
He proved he was made of sterner stuff.
他证明了自己更有胆识。

10. a diamond in the rough · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:42:42
尚未打磨但很有潜力的人或事物
字幕中出现：a diamond in the rough
也作 a rough diamond；比喻外表普通、品质或潜力出众。
The empty shop is a diamond in the rough.
这家空店铺很有改造潜力。
Our newest recruit is a diamond in the rough.
新招的人是块有待雕琢的璞玉。
She saw a diamond in the rough where others saw trouble.
别人只看到麻烦，她却看到了潜力。
