风骚律师逐集英语学习册 · S02E03 Amarillo
英文梗概（76 words，含剧透）
Jimmy approaches Sandpiper residents during a bus trip and finds an effective way to recruit clients. He then makes a television commercial and airs it without obtaining approval from the Davis & Main partners. The advertisement generates calls, but its success does not erase the breach of trust. Kim faces consequences at HHM because of her association with him. Separately, Mike considers a job involving Tuco and begins looking for a way to act without committing murder.

重点词汇：9 项（单词与词组合计）

01. damages · 关键词 · 回看 00:17:46
损害赔偿金
字幕中出现：damages
法律义通常用复数；damage 常指损害本身，注意区别。
The court awarded damages to the customer.
法院判给客户损害赔偿金。
They are seeking damages for the loss.
他们正就损失请求赔偿。
The storm caused damage, but the claim is for damages.
暴风雨造成了损害，而索赔请求的是赔偿金。

02. disbarment · 关键词 · 回看 00:11:18
取消律师执业资格
字幕中出现：disbarred
比暂时 suspension 更严重；动词 disbar。
The misconduct could lead to disbarment.
不当行为可能导致吊销律师资格。
He faced disbarment after falsifying records.
他因伪造记录面临取消执业资格。
Disbarment ended her legal practice.
取消资格终结了她的律师执业。

03. solicitation · 关键词 · 回看 00:07:42
招揽；请求；教唆或邀约
字幕中出现：solicitation
法律中可指招揽客户或某类犯罪邀约；须看宾语和场景。
The rules restrict direct solicitation of clients.
规则限制直接招揽客户。
The complaint concerned improper solicitation.
投诉涉及不当招揽行为。
They received a solicitation for donations.
他们收到了募捐请求。

04. deposition · 关键词 · 回看 00:10:38
庭外宣誓证言；取证问询
字幕中出现：deposition
美国诉讼术语，通常在庭前进行并记录，不等于普通访谈。
Her deposition lasted four hours.
她的庭外宣誓问询持续了四小时。
Counsel reviewed the deposition transcript.
律师审阅了证言笔录。
He contradicted his earlier deposition.
他的说法与先前的宣誓证言矛盾。

05. nebulous · 关键词 · 回看 00:18:11
模糊不清的；含糊的
字幕中出现：nebulous
常指概念、计划、理由不够清晰。
The proposal remained nebulous.
提案仍然含糊不清。
He offered a nebulous explanation.
他给出了模糊的解释。
Their goals were too nebulous to measure.
他们的目标太模糊，无法衡量。

06. discrepancy · 关键词 · 回看 00:02:53
不一致；差异
字幕中出现：discrepancy
常指两份记录、说法或数字不符。
There is a discrepancy between the two invoices.
两张发票存在差异。
The audit found several discrepancies.
审计发现了几处不一致。
Can you explain the discrepancy?
你能解释这个出入吗？

07. intimidation · 关键词 · 回看 00:32:01
恐吓；威逼
字幕中出现：intimidation
动词 intimidate；与合法的强硬谈判并非自动等同。
The witness reported intimidation.
证人报告遭到恐吓。
They ruled out tactics based on intimidation.
他们排除了以威吓为基础的策略。
She refused to give in to intimidation.
她拒绝向恐吓屈服。

08. on the line · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:11:56
面临风险；处于通话中
字幕中出现：on the line
声誉、工作等 on the line 指可能失去；电话语境指在线。
My reputation is on the line.
我的声誉面临风险。
Is the client still on the line?
客户还在线上吗？
There's a lot of money on the line.
有一大笔钱面临风险。

09. out on a limb · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:12:18
冒险表态或行动
字幕中出现：out on a limb
go out on a limb，常指缺乏支持却为某人担风险。
She went out on a limb to recommend him.
她冒险为他作了推荐。
I'm going out on a limb with this prediction.
我作这个预测是在冒险。
Don't leave your colleague out on a limb.
别让同事孤立无援地承担风险。
