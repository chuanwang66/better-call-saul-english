01. malpractice · 关键词 · 回看 00:46:14
美式 IPA：/mælˈpræktɪs/
词性：noun
English definition: Professional negligence or misconduct that harms a client or patient.
专业失当；专业过失
字幕中出现：malpractice
常见 medical/legal malpractice；不局限于医生。
The client filed a malpractice claim.
客户提出了专业过失索赔。
Her policy covers legal malpractice.
她的保单承保律师专业过失。
An unfavourable result alone does not prove malpractice.
结果不利本身并不能证明专业失当。

02. precedent · 关键词 · 回看 00:22:59
美式 IPA：/ˈprɛsɪdənt/
词性：noun
English definition: An earlier decision or event used as a guide for later similar situations.
先例；具有参考或约束意义的判例
字幕中出现：precedent
set a precedent 开创先例；与 president 总统区分。
The ruling set a precedent.
裁决开创了先例。
Counsel cited a relevant precedent.
律师引用了相关判例。
There was no clear precedent for the decision.
这个决定没有明确先例。

03. ballistic · 关键词 · 回看 00:46:22
美式 IPA：/bəˈlɪstɪk/
词性：adjective (in go ballistic)
English definition: Extremely angry and reacting in an uncontrolled way.
暴怒的；弹道的
字幕中出现：ballistic
go ballistic 是口语“勃然大怒”；ballistic evidence 才是弹道证据。
本集用法：本集采用 go ballistic 的口语义：勃然大怒。
He went ballistic over the missing files.
他因文件丢失而大发雷霆。
The manager went ballistic when the deal failed.
交易失败时经理暴跳如雷。
The report included ballistic evidence.
报告包含弹道证据。

04. surveillance · 关键词 · 回看 00:41:58
美式 IPA：/sɚˈveɪləns/
词性：noun
English definition: Close observation of people, places, or activities, often to gather information.
监视；监控
字幕中出现：surveillance
通常不可数；under surveillance 受到监视。
The building was under surveillance.
建筑受到监视。
They reviewed surveillance footage.
他们查看了监控录像。
Covert surveillance revealed the meeting.
秘密监视发现了那次会面。

05. out of line · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:37:41
词性：adjective / adverb phrase
English definition: Inappropriate or beyond the limits of acceptable behavior or authority.
言行越界；不合适
字幕中出现：out of line
be out of line 通常是对行为的批评，不是排队的位置。
That comment was out of line.
那句话太过分了。
He admitted he had been out of line.
他承认自己言行越界了。
Tell me if you think I'm out of line.
如果你觉得我过分了，请告诉我。

06. bar hearing · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:45:33
词性：noun phrase
English definition: A proceeding before a legal professional body concerning a lawyer's fitness, conduct, or right to practice.
律师执业纪律听证
字幕中出现：bar hearing
bar 在此指法律职业及其管理体系；不是酒吧或普通庭审。
The bar hearing examined professional misconduct.
律师纪律听证审查了职业不当行为。
He prepared witnesses for the bar hearing.
他为律师纪律听证准备证人。
A bar hearing can affect a lawyer's licence.
律师纪律听证可能影响律师的执照。

07. inventory · 关键词 · 回看 00:41:51
美式 IPA：/ˈɪnvənˌtɔri/
词性：noun / verb
English definition: Goods held in stock or a detailed list of items; to make such a list.
库存；盘点
字幕中出现：inventory
take/do inventory 盘点；也可作动词清点。
They do inventory every month.
他们每月盘点一次。
The missing inventory raised concerns.

She inventoried the office equipment.
她清点了办公设备。
