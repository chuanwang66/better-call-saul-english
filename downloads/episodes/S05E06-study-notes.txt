关键词采用常见美式读法的宽式 IPA；/ɝ/、/ɚ/ 表示带 r 色彩的元音，/i/、/u/ 不另加长音符号。音标对应显示的词条形式。词性列出本页讲解的用法；部分词有其他读法或词性。

01. settlement · 关键词 · 回看 00:37:19
美式 IPA：/ˈsɛtəlmənt/
词性：noun
English definition: An agreement resolving a dispute, or the money paid under that agreement.
和解；和解协议或款项
字幕中出现：settlement
诉讼中常指双方协议解决争议；不等于法院判决。
The parties reached a settlement.
双方达成了和解。
They rejected the first settlement offer.
他们拒绝了第一次和解报价。
Is the settlement in writing?
和解协议有书面形式吗？

02. immunity · 关键词 · 回看 00:47:05
美式 IPA：/ɪˈmjunəti/
词性：noun
English definition: Protection from prosecution or liability in a legal setting; also resistance to disease.
免疫；法律追诉豁免
字幕中出现：immunity
immunity from prosecution 为免于追诉；具体范围依协议和规则。
The witness requested immunity from prosecution.
证人要求获得追诉豁免。
The agreement did not grant blanket immunity.
协议没有给予全面豁免。
Vaccination can build immunity.
接种疫苗可以建立免疫力。

03. injunction · 关键词 · 回看 00:40:11
美式 IPA：/ɪnˈdʒʌŋkʃən/
词性：noun
English definition: A court order requiring someone to do or stop doing a specified act.
法院禁令
字幕中出现：injunction
常要求停止某行为，也可要求实施某行为；不是一般律师警告函。
The court issued an injunction.
法院发出了禁令。
They sought an injunction against construction.
他们申请禁令阻止施工。
Violating the injunction had serious consequences.
违反禁令产生了严重后果。

04. plausible · 关键词 · 回看 00:49:20
美式 IPA：/ˈplɔzəbəl/
词性：adjective
English definition: Seeming reasonable or believable, even if not yet proved true.
看似合理、可信的
字幕中出现：plausible
比 true 弱，合理外观不等于真实；plausible explanation。
He offered a plausible explanation.
他给出了一个看似合理的解释。
The story was plausible but false.
故事听着可信，却是假的。
We need to consider other plausible causes.
我们需要考虑其他合理的可能原因。

05. infringement · 关键词 · 回看 00:39:50
美式 IPA：/ɪnˈfrɪndʒmənt/
词性：noun
English definition: An unauthorized interference with a right, especially an intellectual property right.
侵权；侵犯
字幕中出现：infringement
infringement of a right；常见 copyright/trademark infringement。
The claim concerned trademark infringement.
诉求涉及商标侵权。
They denied any infringement of her rights.
他们否认侵犯了她的权利。
Counsel reviewed the alleged infringement.
律师审查了被指称的侵权行为。

06. compensate · 关键词 · 回看 00:43:05
美式 IPA：/ˈkɑmpənˌseɪt/
词性：verb
English definition: To provide payment or another benefit for loss, harm, or work; to make up for a disadvantage.
赔偿；补偿；弥补
字幕中出现：compensated
compensate someone for a loss；compensate for a weakness。
The company compensated her for the damage.
公司赔偿了她的损失。
Experience can compensate for limited resources.
经验可以弥补资源不足。
Nothing could fully compensate him for the lost time.
没有什么能完全补偿他失去的时间。

07. blackmail · 关键词 · 回看 00:39:08
美式 IPA：/ˈblækˌmeɪl/
词性：noun / verb
English definition: Demanding money or cooperation by threatening to expose damaging information; to make such a demand.
敲诈勒索；以曝光信息等相威胁
字幕中出现：blackmail
名词或动词；日常 emotional blackmail 指情感勒索。
She reported the blackmail attempt.
她举报了那次敲诈企图。
He refused to give in to blackmail.
他拒绝向敲诈低头。
Guilt can be used as a form of emotional blackmail.
内疚感可能被用作情感勒索的手段。

08. under oath · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:49:17
词性：adverb phrase
English definition: Having formally promised to tell the truth in a setting where a false statement may have legal consequences.
在宣誓后；在誓言约束下
字幕中出现：under oath
法律表达；描述证言须真实这一正式要求。
The witness answered the questions under oath.
证人宣誓后回答了问题。
She gave that statement under oath.
她在宣誓后作出了那份陈述。
Were you under oath when you said that?
你说那句话时是在宣誓作证吗？

09. in cahoots · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:48:05
词性：adjective phrase / idiom
English definition: Working together secretly, usually for a dishonest purpose.
暗中勾结的
字幕中出现：in cahoots
口语，be in cahoots with someone，带贬义。
The two suppliers were in cahoots.
两家供应商暗中勾结。
She suspected he was in cahoots with the buyer.
她怀疑他与买家串通。
They denied being in cahoots.
他们否认暗中勾结。

10. a bone to pick · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:19:20
词性：idiom
English definition: A complaint or disagreement that one wants to discuss with someone.
有不满要与某人理论
字幕中出现：a bone to pick
have a bone to pick with someone；通常是抱怨或质问，不是实际争夺骨头。
I have a bone to pick with the supplier.
我有件不满的事要找供应商理论。
She had a bone to pick with her manager.
她对经理有意见要说。
You sound like you have a bone to pick with me.
听起来你有事要找我算账。
