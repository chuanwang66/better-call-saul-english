01. felony · 关键词 · 回看 00:55:51
美式 IPA：/ˈfɛləni/
词性：noun
English definition: A serious category of crime, generally carrying more severe penalties than a misdemeanor.
重罪
字幕中出现：felony
美国刑事分类用语；具体定义和后果依司法辖区及法律而定。
He was charged with a felony.
他被控犯有重罪。
The form asks about felony convictions.
表格询问是否有重罪定罪记录。
The lawyer explained the difference between a felony and a misdemeanor.
律师解释了重罪和轻罪的区别。

02. discovery · 关键词 · 回看 00:19:21
美式 IPA：/dɪˈskʌvɚi/
词性：noun
English definition: Finding something previously unknown; in litigation, the process of obtaining and exchanging case information.
发现；诉讼中的证据开示
字幕中出现：discovery
法律义是审前依法获取对方相关信息的程序，区别于普通“发现”。
The parties exchanged documents during discovery.
双方在证据开示阶段交换了文件。
The lawyer requested more time for discovery.
律师申请延长证据开示时间。
The discovery of a leak changed our schedule.
发现漏水后，我们改变了日程。（普通义。）

03. affidavit · 关键词 · 回看 00:19:29
美式 IPA：/ˌæfəˈdeɪvɪt/
词性：noun
English definition: A written statement that its maker formally swears is true.
宣誓书；宣誓陈述
字幕中出现：affidavits
通常指经宣誓作出的书面事实陈述；不是普通便条或无条件的真实性证明。
She signed an affidavit describing what she saw.
她签署了描述所见情况的宣誓书。
The lawyer attached an affidavit to the filing.
律师在提交材料中附上了宣誓书。
Does the affidavit include the date of the incident?
宣誓书包含事件日期吗？

04. fraud · 关键词 · 回看 00:54:51
美式 IPA：/frɔd/
词性：noun
English definition: Deliberate deception intended to obtain a benefit or cause another person's loss.
欺诈
字幕中出现：fraud
法律或商务语境指欺骗行为；a fraud 也可指骗子或冒牌者。
The bank investigated possible fraud.
银行调查了可能的欺诈行为。
The company introduced fraud prevention training.
公司开展了防欺诈培训。
She realized the offer was a fraud.
她意识到那个邀约是骗局。

05. malpractice · 关键词 · 回看 00:19:02
美式 IPA：/mælˈpræktɪs/
词性：noun
English definition: Professional negligence or misconduct that harms a client or patient.
专业失当；专业过失
字幕中出现：malpractice
常见 medical/legal malpractice；不局限于医生。
The client filed a malpractice claim.
客户提出了专业过失索赔。
Her policy covers legal malpractice.
她的保单承保律师专业过失。
An unfavourable result alone does not prove malpractice.
结果不利本身并不能证明专业失当。

06. conviction · 关键词 · 回看 00:26:08
美式 IPA：/kənˈvɪkʃən/
词性：noun
English definition: A finding of guilt in a criminal case; also a firmly held belief.
定罪；坚定信念
字幕中出现：conviction
法律中指被判有罪；with conviction 表示坚定地。
He appealed his conviction.
他对定罪提出上诉。
The conviction was later overturned.
定罪后来被推翻。
She spoke with conviction.
她说得坚定有力。

07. privilege · 关键词 · 回看 00:26:52
美式 IPA：/ˈprɪvəlɪdʒ/
词性：noun
English definition: A special right or advantage; in law, a protection that can prevent compelled disclosure of certain communications.
法律保密特权；特殊权利或荣幸
字幕中出现：privileged
法律中可指受保护、不须披露的沟通；不同于一般 confidentiality。
Counsel raised a claim of privilege.
律师主张适用保密特权。
The conversation may be protected by privilege.
谈话可能受保密特权保护。
Access to the archive is a privilege.
查阅档案是一项特殊待遇。

08. exonerate · 关键词 · 回看 00:17:13

English definition: To clear someone of blame, responsibility, or an accusation.
洗清嫌疑；免除责任
字幕中出现：exonerate

The recording exonerated the driver.
录音洗清了司机的嫌疑。
An independent review exonerated her.
独立审查免除了她的责任。
The evidence did not fully exonerate him.
证据没有完全洗清他的嫌疑。

09. embezzle · 关键词 · 回看 00:24:07

侵吞受托管理的钱财
字幕中出现：embezzled

员工侵吞了公司资金。
He was accused of embezzling pension money.
他被控侵吞养老金。
The audit checked whether funds had been embezzled.
审计核查资金是否遭到侵吞。

10. allegation · 关键词 · 回看 00:26:12
美式 IPA：/ˌæləˈɡeɪʃən/
词性：noun
English definition: A claim of wrongdoing that has not yet been established as true.
尚未经证实的指控或说法
字幕中出现：allegations
allegation of misconduct；避免把 allegation 当作已确认事实。
The company denied the allegation.
公司否认了这项指控。
An allegation is not a conviction.
指控不等于定罪。
Investigators examined allegations of bribery.
调查人员审查了有关行贿的指控。

11. on the record · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:25:25
词性：adjective / adverb phrase
English definition: Officially recorded or available to be publicly attributed to the speaker.
可公开引用的；正式记录在案的
字幕中出现：on the record
常见于采访、调查和会议；与 off the record 相对。
Is this conversation on the record?
这段谈话可以公开引用吗？
She put her objection on the record.
她正式提出异议并要求记录在案。
The spokesperson agreed to speak on the record.
发言人同意以可公开引用的方式发言。

12. conflict of interest · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:25:50

利益冲突
字幕中出现：conflict of interest

Please disclose any conflict of interest.
请披露任何利益冲突。
She stepped aside because of a conflict of interest.
她因利益冲突而退出。
Could accepting this gift create a conflict of interest?
收这份礼物会产生利益冲突吗？

13. class action · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:08:42

字幕中出现：class action
由代表成员为具有共同问题的一类人提起的诉讼；资格须依法认定。
The customers filed a class action.
顾客提起了集体诉讼。
The court considered whether to certify the class action.
法院审议是否批准集体诉讼资格。
The notice explains who is included in the class action.
通知解释了哪些人属于集体诉讼成员。
