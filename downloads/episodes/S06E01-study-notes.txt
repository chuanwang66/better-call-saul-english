Earlier, Jimmy and Kim begin their campaign to damage Howard's reputation, planting the suggestion of drug use at his golf club. Nacho hides after the failed assassination and relies on instructions that may not protect him. Lalo conceals his survival while preparing his response. The season opens with carefully managed appearances, as several characters work from incomplete or deliberately false information.

关键词采用常见美式读法的宽式 IPA；/ɝ/、/ɚ/ 表示带 r 色彩的元音，/i/、/u/ 不另加长音符号。音标对应显示的词条形式。词性列出本页讲解的用法；部分词有其他读法或词性。

01. bail · 关键词 · 回看 00:23:06
美式 IPA：/beɪl/
词性：noun / verb
English definition: Conditional release before trial, sometimes secured by money; to secure someone's release in this way.
保释；保释金
字幕中出现：bail
常用 on bail 或 post bail；保释不表示无罪。
The defendant was released on bail.
被告获准保释。
The family helped him post bail.
家人帮助他缴纳保释金。
The judge considered the request for bail.
法官审议了保释申请。

02. assault · 关键词 · 回看 00:26:51
美式 IPA：/əˈsɔlt/
词性：noun / verb
English definition: A physical attack or, in some legal uses, conduct that creates fear of imminent harm; to attack.
袭击；人身攻击
字幕中出现：assault
日常义较宽；具体法律构成因辖区不同，不与 battery 简单等同。
The police investigated the alleged assault.
警方调查了涉嫌袭击事件。
The witness described an assault near the station.
证人描述了车站附近的一起袭击。
The lawyer reviewed the assault charge.
律师审查了袭击指控。

03. preliminary · 关键词 · 回看 00:13:00
美式 IPA：/prɪˈlɪməˌnɛri/
词性：adjective
English definition: Taking place before the main action or final decision.
初步的；正式程序之前的
字幕中出现：preliminary
preliminary hearing 初步听证；不等于最终结果。
The preliminary findings were inconclusive.
初步结果尚无定论。
A preliminary hearing was scheduled.
初步听证已经安排。
They held preliminary discussions.
他们进行了初步讨论。

04. prosecutorial · 关键词 · 回看 00:24:04
美式 IPA：/ˌprɑsɪkjuˈtɔriəl/
词性：adjective
English definition: Relating to a prosecutor or the work of bringing criminal charges.
检方的；公诉职能相关的
字幕中出现：Prosecutorial
形容词，常见 prosecutorial misconduct/discretion。
The complaint alleged prosecutorial misconduct.
投诉指称存在检方不当行为。
The decision involved prosecutorial discretion.
决定涉及检方裁量权。
They debated prosecutorial independence.
他们讨论了检方的独立性。

05. misconduct · 关键词 · 回看 00:24:04
美式 IPA：/mɪsˈkɑndʌkt/
词性：noun
English definition: Behavior that violates rules, duties, or expected standards.
不当行为；违规失职
字幕中出现：misconduct
通常不可数；professional misconduct 职业不当行为。
The panel investigated professional misconduct.
小组调查了职业不当行为。
He was dismissed for serious misconduct.
他因严重违规被解雇。
The complaint alleged prosecutorial misconduct.
投诉指称检方存在不当行为。

06. blackmail · 关键词 · 回看 00:41:55
美式 IPA：/ˈblækˌmeɪl/
词性：noun / verb
English definition: Demanding money or cooperation by threatening to expose damaging information; to make such a demand.
敲诈勒索；以曝光信息等相威胁
字幕中出现：blackmailing
名词或动词；日常 emotional blackmail 指情感勒索。
She reported the blackmail attempt.
她举报了那次敲诈企图。
He refused to give in to blackmail.
他拒绝向敲诈低头。
Guilt can be used as a form of emotional blackmail.
内疚感可能被用作情感勒索的手段。

07. mercenary · 关键词 · 回看 00:20:14
美式 IPA：/ˈmɝsəˌnɛri/
词性：noun
English definition: A fighter hired to take part in armed operations for payment.
雇佣兵；唯利是图的
字幕中出现：mercenaries
名词指受雇作战者；形容词指只为钱行事。
本集用法：本集指受雇执行武装行动的人，采用雇佣兵这一名词义。
The report mentioned foreign mercenaries.
报告提到外国雇佣兵。
His interest in the project was mercenary.
他对项目的兴趣完全出于金钱。
They questioned the mercenary's loyalty.
他们质疑雇佣兵的忠诚。

08. public defender · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:26:37
词性：noun phrase
English definition: A publicly funded lawyer who represents eligible defendants unable to afford counsel.
公设辩护律师
字幕中出现：public defender
美国语境常指为符合条件、无力聘律师的被告提供辩护的律师。
A public defender represented the defendant.
一名公设辩护律师代表被告。
She works as a public defender.
她担任公设辩护律师。
The public defender asked for more time to prepare.
公设辩护律师申请更多准备时间。
