重点词汇：6 项（单词与词组合计）
关键词采用常见美式读法的宽式 IPA；/ɝ/、/ɚ/ 表示带 r 色彩的元音，/i/、/u/ 不另加长音符号。音标对应显示的词条形式。词性列出本页讲解的用法；部分词有其他读法或词性。

01. probation · 关键词 · 回看 00:18:04
美式 IPA：/proʊˈbeɪʃən/
词性：noun
English definition: Court-ordered community supervision under conditions; also a trial period for a new employee.
缓刑或社区监督；试用期
字幕中出现：probation
刑事含义按具体制度判断；工作语境可指试用期。
The sentence included a period of probation.
判决包含一段社区监督期。
She must comply with the conditions of probation.
她必须遵守监督条件。
My probation at the new job ends next month.
我新工作的试用期下个月结束。

02. prosecution · 关键词 · 回看 00:20:35
美式 IPA：/ˌprɑsəˈkjuʃən/
词性：noun
English definition: The pursuit of criminal charges in court; also the lawyers bringing those charges.
控方；起诉；检控
字幕中出现：prosecution
the prosecution 常指刑事案件中的控方。
The prosecution presented its evidence.
控方出示了证据。
The defense challenged the prosecution's account.
辩方质疑了控方的说法。
The file was sent for prosecution.
案卷被移送起诉。

03. fabricate · 关键词 · 回看 00:45:05
美式 IPA：/ˈfæbrɪˌkeɪt/
词性：verb
English definition: To invent a false story or evidence; also to manufacture something.
捏造；制造
字幕中出现：fabricate
fabricate evidence/story 为贬义；fabricate parts 为中性制造。
He fabricated an excuse.
他编造了一个借口。
The witness denied fabricating evidence.
证人否认伪造证据。
The workshop fabricates metal components.
车间制造金属零件。

04. on the record · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:18:41
词性：adjective / adverb phrase
English definition: Officially recorded or available to be publicly attributed to the speaker.
可公开引用的；正式记录在案的
字幕中出现：on the record
常见于采访、调查和会议；与 off the record 相对。
Is this conversation on the record?
这段谈话可以公开引用吗？
She put her objection on the record.
她正式提出异议并要求记录在案。
The spokesperson agreed to speak on the record.
发言人同意以可公开引用的方式发言。

05. time served · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:18:04
词性：noun phrase
English definition: Time already spent in custody that counts toward a sentence, or a sentence limited to that time.
已服刑的时间；以已羁押时间抵刑
字幕中出现：time served
常见 sentenced to time served；不表示无罪。
He was sentenced to time served.
他被判以已服刑时间抵作刑期。
The court credited her with time served.
法院折抵了她已被羁押的时间。
The agreement recommended time served.
协议建议以已服刑时间处理。

06. probation officer · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:20:58
词性：noun phrase
English definition: An official who supervises people placed on probation and reports on their compliance.
缓刑监管官
字幕中出现：probation officer
监督缓刑或相关社区监管条件的履行；不是监狱看守。
She met her probation officer weekly.
她每周见缓刑监管官。
The probation officer reviewed his progress.
缓刑监管官审查了他的进展。
He reported the address change to his probation officer.
他向缓刑监管官报告了地址变化。
