风骚律师逐集英语学习册 · S02E02 Cobbler
英文梗概（77 words，含剧透）
Jimmy settles into Davis & Main, where an expensive office and professional support come with expectations about his conduct. Mike asks him to help Pryce, whose report of stolen baseball cards has raised police suspicions. Jimmy invents an embarrassing explanation involving private videos to divert the detectives from the real crime. The story works, but Kim is disturbed when she learns what he did. His ingenuity solves an immediate problem while creating a deeper disagreement about professional ethics.

重点词汇：8 项（单词与词组合计）

01. discovery · 关键词 · 回看 00:07:35
发现；诉讼中的证据开示
字幕中出现：discovery
法律义是审前依法获取对方相关信息的程序，区别于普通“发现”。
The parties exchanged documents during discovery.
双方在证据开示阶段交换了文件。
The lawyer requested more time for discovery.
律师申请延长证据开示时间。
The discovery of a leak changed our schedule.
发现漏水后，我们改变了日程。（普通义。）

02. subpoena · 关键词 · 回看 00:31:22
传票；以传票要求出庭或提交材料
字幕中出现：subpoena
法律名词也可作动词；不等同于 arrest warrant 逮捕令。
The witness received a subpoena.
证人收到了传票。
The court issued a subpoena for the records.
法院发出传票，要求提交记录。
The lawyer sought to subpoena another witness.
律师申请以传票传唤另一名证人。

03. disbarment · 关键词 · 回看 00:45:06
取消律师执业资格
字幕中出现：disbarment
比暂时 suspension 更严重；动词 disbar。
The misconduct could lead to disbarment.
不当行为可能导致吊销律师资格。
He faced disbarment after falsifying records.
他因伪造记录面临取消执业资格。
Disbarment ended her legal practice.
取消资格终结了她的律师执业。

04. exonerate · 关键词 · 回看 00:44:36
洗清嫌疑；免除责任
字幕中出现：exonerate
exonerate someone；不总与法院 acquit 同义。
The recording exonerated the driver.
录音洗清了司机的嫌疑。
An independent review exonerated her.
独立审查免除了她的责任。
The evidence did not fully exonerate him.
证据没有完全洗清他的嫌疑。

05. falsify · 关键词 · 回看 00:44:38
伪造；篡改
字幕中出现：falsified
falsify records；研究中也可指证伪理论，须分语境。
The manager falsified expense records.
经理篡改了费用记录。
They were accused of falsifying signatures.
他们被控伪造签名。
One counterexample may falsify the hypothesis.
一个反例可能证伪该假说。

06. volatile · 关键词 · 回看 00:39:39
易波动的；易激动或失控的
字幕中出现：volatile
金融、性格、化学中含义不同；volatile situation 不稳定局面。
The market became volatile.
市场变得波动剧烈。
He had a volatile temper.
他脾气易爆。
The situation remained volatile.
局势仍不稳定。

07. fabricate · 关键词 · 回看 00:44:33
捏造；制造
字幕中出现：fabricated
fabricate evidence/story 为贬义；fabricate parts 为中性制造。
He fabricated an excuse.
他编造了一个借口。
The witness denied fabricating evidence.
证人否认伪造证据。
The workshop fabricates metal components.
车间制造金属零件。

08. brass tacks · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:37:40
实际问题；关键细节
字幕中出现：brass tacks
通常用于 get down to brass tacks，进入实质讨论。
Let's get down to brass tacks: what will it cost?
我们谈实际问题吧：要花多少钱？
After lunch, we got down to brass tacks.
午饭后我们开始谈实质问题。
She wanted brass tacks, not another presentation.
她要的是实际细节，不是又一次演示。
