风骚律师逐集英语学习册 · S06E06 Axe and Grind
英文梗概（74 words，含剧透）
Jimmy and Kim prepare photographs and a drugged encounter to sabotage the Sandpiper mediation. A detail about the mediator's appearance threatens the plan, and Kim abandons a professional opportunity to help repair it. A flashback shows an early lesson in deception from her mother. Lalo pursues another member of Werner's construction crew to uncover Gus's operation. The episode brings both plots close to execution while showing the alternatives the characters choose to leave behind.

01. felony · 关键词 · 回看 00:14:58
重罪
字幕中出现：felony
美国刑事分类用语；具体定义和后果依司法辖区及法律而定。
He was charged with a felony.
他被控犯有重罪。
The form asks about felony convictions.
表格询问是否有重罪定罪记录。
The lawyer explained the difference between a felony and a misdemeanor.
律师解释了重罪和轻罪的区别。

被告获准保释。

家人帮助他缴纳保释金。
The judge considered the request for bail.
法官审议了保释申请。

律师引用了相关法条。
The claim depends on how the statute is interpreted.
诉求取决于如何解释该法令。

They proposed an amendment to the bill.
他们提议修改法案。
The Fourth Amendment concerns searches and seizures.
第四修正案涉及搜查与扣押。
