英文梗概（71 words，含剧透）
The Kettlemans disappear, and Nacho becomes the main suspect. Jimmy agrees to represent him and learns that Nacho's presence near the house may expose a separate crime. Believing the family staged its own disappearance, Jimmy searches for them instead of accepting the kidnapping theory. He finds the Kettlemans camping near their home with the missing money. Meanwhile, Mike helps Jimmy think through the case, beginning an uneasy professional connection between them.

关键词采用常见美式读法的宽式 IPA；/ɝ/、/ɚ/ 表示带 r 色彩的元音，/i/、/u/ 不另加长音符号。音标对应显示的词条形式。词性列出本页讲解的用法；部分词有其他读法或词性。

01. defendant · 关键词 · 回看 00:15:07
美式 IPA：/dɪˈfɛndənt/
词性：noun
English definition: The person or organization against whom a criminal charge or civil claim is brought.
被告
字幕中出现：defendant
可用于刑事或民事案件；不是所有案件里的被告都已被定罪。
The defendant entered the courtroom.
被告走进法庭。
The defendant denied the allegation.
被告否认了指控。
How many defendants are named in the case?
这个案件列了多少名被告？

02. assault · 关键词 · 回看 00:01:37
美式 IPA：/əˈsɔlt/
词性：noun / verb
English definition: A physical attack or, in some legal uses, conduct that creates fear of imminent harm; to attack.
袭击；人身攻击
字幕中出现：assault
日常义较宽；具体法律构成因辖区不同，不与 battery 简单等同。
The police investigated the alleged assault.
警方调查了涉嫌袭击事件。
The witness described an assault near the station.
证人描述了车站附近的一起袭击。
The lawyer reviewed the assault charge.
律师审查了袭击指控。

03. incriminate · 关键词 · 回看 00:35:51
美式 IPA：/ɪnˈkrɪməˌneɪt/
词性：verb
English definition: To provide information that makes someone appear involved in a crime.
使显得有罪；提供不利涉罪证据
字幕中出现：incriminating
incriminate oneself；与 implicate 相近但更直接涉及犯罪证据。
The recording could incriminate him.
录音可能成为他涉罪的证据。
She refused to incriminate herself.
她拒绝作出自证其罪的陈述。
Investigators found incriminating documents.
调查人员发现了不利的涉罪文件。

04. confidentiality · 关键词 · 回看 00:34:16
美式 IPA：/ˌkɑnfəˌdɛnʃiˈæləti/
词性：noun
English definition: The duty or practice of keeping sensitive information from unauthorized disclosure.
保密性；保密义务
字幕中出现：confidentiality
与法律 privilege 区分；保密承诺不总等于可以拒绝作证。
Confidentiality is essential to this work.
保密对这项工作至关重要。
They signed a confidentiality agreement.
他们签署了保密协议。
The breach of confidentiality damaged trust.
泄密损害了信任。

05. remorse · 关键词 · 回看 00:26:18
美式 IPA：/rɪˈmɔrs/
词性：noun
English definition: Deep regret and guilt about harm one has caused.
懊悔；对过错的内疚
字幕中出现：remorse
show remorse；比单纯 regret 更突出对伤害或过错的道德反省。
He showed genuine remorse.
他表现出真诚的悔意。
She felt remorse for deceiving her friend.
她因欺骗朋友感到内疚。
The apology contained little remorse.
道歉中几乎没有悔意。

06. embezzle · 关键词 · 回看 00:38:00

词性：verb
English definition: To dishonestly take money or property that one was trusted to manage.
侵吞受托管理的钱财
字幕中出现：embezzled

员工侵吞了公司资金。
He was accused of embezzling pension money.
他被控侵吞养老金。
The audit checked whether funds had been embezzled.
审计核查资金是否遭到侵吞。

07. off the hook · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:39:39
词性：idiom
English definition: Freed from responsibility or trouble; with ring, describing a phone ringing repeatedly.
摆脱责任或困境；电话响个不停
字幕中出现：off the hook
let someone off the hook 放某人一马；ring off the hook 形容电话接连不断地响。
The apology didn't let him off the hook.
道歉并没有让他摆脱责任。
You're not off the hook for the bill.
你还得承担这笔账单。
The phones were ringing off the hook.
电话接连不断地响。

08. attorney-client privilege · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:29:49
词性：noun phrase
English definition: A legal protection for certain confidential communications between a lawyer and client made for legal advice.
律师与委托人之间的保密沟通特权
字幕中出现：attorney-client privilege
法律术语；保护范围、例外依具体法律而定，不等于所有与律师有关的信息都保密。
The lawyer raised attorney-client privilege.
律师提出律师与委托人保密沟通特权。
The court considered whether attorney-client privilege applied.
法院审议该沟通是否受这一特权保护。
The training explains the limits of attorney-client privilege.
培训解释了律师与委托人保密沟通特权的界限。

09. dead to rights · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:38:03
词性：adverb phrase / idiom
English definition: In a situation where clear evidence establishes someone's wrongdoing.
有确凿把柄；当场被抓
字幕中出现：dead to rights
have/catch someone dead to rights，主要见于美式口语。
They caught him dead to rights with the stolen laptop.
他拿着赃物笔记本，被抓个正着。
The video gave them the suspect dead to rights.
录像让他们掌握了嫌疑人的确凿把柄。
You think you've got me dead to rights?
你以为已经抓到我的铁证了吗？

10. on your own recognizance · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:45:52
词性：adverb phrase
English definition: Released before trial on a personal promise to return to court, without posting bail money.
以个人保证获释，无须交保释金
字幕中出现：on your own recognizance
美国法律术语；人称可变，不表示没有出庭义务。
She was released on her own recognizance.
她凭个人保证获释。
The court considered release on his own recognizance.
法院考虑让他凭个人保证获释。
Release on your own recognizance still requires court attendance.
凭个人保证获释仍须按要求出庭。

11. time served · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:14:01

他被判以已服刑时间抵作刑期。
The court credited her with time served.
法院折抵了她已被羁押的时间。
