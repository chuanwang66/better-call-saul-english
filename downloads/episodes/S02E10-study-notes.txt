英文梗概（72 words，含剧透）
Chuck recovers after his collapse and presents himself as unable to continue practising law. Worried about his brother, Jimmy admits to altering the Mesa Verde documents. 01. felony · 关键词 · 回看 00:46:55
美式 IPA：/ˈfɛləni/
词性：noun
English definition: A serious category of crime, generally carrying more severe penalties than a misdemeanor.
重罪
字幕中出现：felony
美国刑事分类用语；具体定义和后果依司法辖区及法律而定。
He was charged with a felony.
他被控犯有重罪。
The form asks about felony convictions.
表格询问是否有重罪定罪记录。
The lawyer explained the difference between a felony and a misdemeanor.
律师解释了重罪和轻罪的区别。

02. disbarment · 关键词 · 回看 00:42:34
美式 IPA：/dɪsˈbɑrmənt/
词性：noun
English definition: The removal of a lawyer's authorization to practice law.
取消律师执业资格
字幕中出现：disbarred
比暂时 suspension 更严重；动词 disbar。
The misconduct could lead to disbarment.
不当行为可能导致吊销律师资格。
He faced disbarment after falsifying records.
他因伪造记录面临取消执业资格。
Disbarment ended her legal practice.
取消资格终结了她的律师执业。

03. guardianship · 关键词 · 回看 00:10:18
美式 IPA：/ˈɡɑrdiənˌʃɪp/
词性：noun
English definition: Legal responsibility for caring for another person or managing their affairs.
监护权；监护安排
字幕中出现：Guardianship

法院审查了监护安排。
