英文梗概（73 words，含剧透）
Jimmy applies to regain his law licence but is rejected because the panel finds him insincere. His failure leads to an argument with Kim about support, responsibility and how others see him. Kim eventually agrees to help with an appeal. Mike discovers that Werner has escaped the secure construction site to meet his wife. The security breach creates an urgent search, while Lalo's arrival introduces a rival eager to understand Gus's hidden activities.

01. felony · 关键词 · 回看 00:46:14

词性：noun
English definition: A serious category of crime, generally carrying more severe penalties than a misdemeanor.
重罪
字幕中出现：felony

The form asks about felony convictions.
表格询问是否有重罪定罪记录。
The lawyer explained the difference between a felony and a misdemeanor.
律师解释了重罪和轻罪的区别。
