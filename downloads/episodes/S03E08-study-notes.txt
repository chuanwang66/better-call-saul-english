关键词采用常见美式读法的宽式 IPA；/ɝ/、/ɚ/ 表示带 r 色彩的元音，/i/、/u/ 不另加长音符号。音标对应显示的词条形式。词性列出本页讲解的用法；部分词有其他读法或词性。

01. liability · 关键词 · 回看 00:17:19
美式 IPA：/ˌlaɪəˈbɪləti/
词性：noun
English definition: Legal or financial responsibility; also a debt or something that creates a disadvantage.
法律责任；偿付义务；不利因素
字幕中出现：liability
法律语境指须依法承担的责任；复数 liabilities 也可指负债。
The contract sets out each party's liability.
合同规定了各方的责任。
They disputed liability for the damage.
他们就损害责任发生争议。
The old equipment has become a liability.
旧设备已经成了负担。（比喻义。）

02. damages · 关键词 · 回看 00:43:15
美式 IPA：/ˈdæmɪdʒɪz/
词性：plural noun
English definition: Money awarded to compensate for a legally recognized injury or loss.
原形：damage
损害赔偿金
字幕中出现：damages
法律义通常用复数；damage 常指损害本身，注意区别。
The court awarded damages to the customer.
法院判给客户损害赔偿金。
They are seeking damages for the loss.
他们正就损失请求赔偿。
The storm caused damage, but the claim is for damages.
暴风雨造成了损害，而索赔请求的是赔偿金。

03. trespassing · 关键词 · 回看 00:09:21
美式 IPA：/ˈtrɛsˌpæsɪŋ/
词性：noun / verb (-ing form)
English definition: Entering or remaining on another person's property without permission; the -ing form of trespass.
原形：trespass
擅自进入或逗留他人土地、场所
字幕中出现：trespassing
常见标牌 No trespassing；不是泛指任何闯祸。
The sign says No trespassing.
牌子上写着禁止擅入。
They were warned about trespassing on private land.
他们被警告不得擅入私人土地。
The complaint concerned trespassing, not theft.
投诉涉及擅自侵入，而非盗窃。

04. litigation · 关键词 · 回看 00:43:44
美式 IPA：/ˌlɪtəˈɡeɪʃən/
词性：noun
English definition: The process of pursuing or defending a dispute through the courts.
诉讼；进行诉讼的过程
字幕中出现：litigation
通常不可数；litigation costs 诉讼费用。
The dispute led to years of litigation.
争议导致多年诉讼。
Litigation can be expensive and slow.
诉讼可能昂贵且漫长。
She works in commercial litigation.
她从事商事诉讼工作。

05. malpractice · 关键词 · 回看 00:32:09
美式 IPA：/mælˈpræktɪs/
词性：noun
English definition: Professional negligence or misconduct that harms a client or patient.
专业失当；专业过失
字幕中出现：malpractice
常见 medical/legal malpractice；不局限于医生。
The client filed a malpractice claim.
客户提出了专业过失索赔。
Her policy covers legal malpractice.
她的保单承保律师专业过失。
An unfavourable result alone does not prove malpractice.
结果不利本身并不能证明专业失当。

06. mitigate · 关键词 · 回看 00:43:15
美式 IPA：/ˈmɪtəˌɡeɪt/
词性：verb
English definition: To reduce the seriousness, severity, or harmful effects of something.
减轻严重程度或损害
字幕中出现：mitigate
mitigate risk/damages；不表示彻底消除。
The team acted to mitigate the damage.
团队采取行动减轻损害。
Training can mitigate operational risk.
培训可以降低运营风险。
The judge considered mitigating circumstances.
法官考虑了可减轻处罚的情节。

07. distress · 关键词 · 回看 00:12:04
美式 IPA：/dɪˈstrɛs/
词性：noun / verb
English definition: Severe emotional suffering or difficulty; to cause such suffering.
严重焦虑、痛苦；困境
字幕中出现：distressed
in distress；distressed 是形容词。
She appeared to be in distress.
她看起来十分焦虑。
The letter caused considerable distress.
那封信让人非常痛苦。
The vessel sent a distress signal.
船只发出了求救信号。
