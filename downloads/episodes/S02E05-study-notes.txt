英文梗概（73 words，含剧透）
A flashback shows how easily Jimmy charms Chuck's wife Rebecca, highlighting the tension between the brothers. In the present, Kim works relentlessly to bring Mesa Verde to HHM, hoping to restore her standing. Winning the client does not immediately free her from punishment. Hector pressures Mike to change his account of Tuco's arrest. Jimmy's effort to navigate office rules and Mike's negotiations both show how powerful people impose conditions on those beneath them.

01. retainer · 关键词 · 回看 00:33:53
聘用律师等专业人士的预付费用；聘用安排
字幕中出现：retainer
具体如何计费取决于协议；不必然指已赚取的费用。
The client paid a retainer before work began.
客户在工作开始前支付了预付聘用费。
Ask what the retainer covers.
问清预付费用覆盖哪些服务。
The firm explained its retainer agreement.
事务所解释了聘用协议。

09. summary judgment · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:23:05
简易判决；无需完整审判作出的判决
字幕中出现：summary judgment
美国民事程序中通常涉及不存在实质事实争议等条件；不是普通“总结”。

The motion argued that summary judgment was appropriate.
