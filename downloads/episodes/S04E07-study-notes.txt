关键词采用常见美式读法的宽式 IPA；/ɝ/、/ɚ/ 表示带 r 色彩的元音，/i/、/u/ 不另加长音符号。音标对应显示的词条形式。词性列出本页讲解的用法；部分词有其他读法或词性。

01. misdemeanor · 关键词 · 回看 00:35:45
美式 IPA：/ˌmɪsdəˈminɚ/
词性：noun
English definition: A criminal offense in a less serious category than a felony.
轻罪
字幕中出现：misdemeanor
美国法律用语；具体分类取决于适用法律，不能只按日常严重程度判断。
The charge was classified as a misdemeanor.
该项指控被归类为轻罪。
She asked whether the offense was a misdemeanor.
她询问该违法行为是否属于轻罪。
The report distinguishes misdemeanors from felonies.
报告区分了轻罪与重罪。

02. probation · 关键词 · 回看 00:36:43
美式 IPA：/proʊˈbeɪʃən/
词性：noun
English definition: Court-ordered community supervision under conditions; also a trial period for a new employee.
缓刑或社区监督；试用期
字幕中出现：probation
刑事含义按具体制度判断；工作语境可指试用期。
The sentence included a period of probation.
判决包含一段社区监督期。
She must comply with the conditions of probation.
她必须遵守监督条件。
My probation at the new job ends next month.
我新工作的试用期下个月结束。

03. bail · 关键词 · 回看 00:38:30
美式 IPA：/beɪl/
词性：noun / verb
English definition: Conditional release before trial, sometimes secured by money; to secure someone's release in this way.
保释；保释金
字幕中出现：bail
常用 on bail 或 post bail；保释不表示无罪。
The defendant was released on bail.
被告获准保释。
The family helped him post bail.
家人帮助他缴纳保释金。
The judge considered the request for bail.
法官审议了保释申请。

04. defendant · 关键词 · 回看 00:36:21
美式 IPA：/dɪˈfɛndənt/
词性：noun
English definition: The person or organization against whom a criminal charge or civil claim is brought.
被告
字幕中出现：defendants
可用于刑事或民事案件；不是所有案件里的被告都已被定罪。
The defendant entered the courtroom.
被告走进法庭。
The defendant denied the allegation.
被告否认了指控。
How many defendants are named in the case?
这个案件列了多少名被告？

05. discovery · 关键词 · 回看 00:06:33
美式 IPA：/dɪˈskʌvɚi/
词性：noun
English definition: Finding something previously unknown; in litigation, the process of obtaining and exchanging case information.
发现；诉讼中的证据开示
字幕中出现：discovery
法律义是审前依法获取对方相关信息的程序，区别于普通“发现”。
The parties exchanged documents during discovery.
双方在证据开示阶段交换了文件。
The lawyer requested more time for discovery.
律师申请延长证据开示时间。
The discovery of a leak changed our schedule.
发现漏水后，我们改变了日程。（普通义。）

06. bribe · 关键词 · 回看 00:35:11
美式 IPA：/braɪb/
词性：noun / verb
English definition: Something offered to improperly influence a decision; to offer such an inducement.
贿赂；贿赂物；行贿
字幕中出现：bribe
可数名词或动词；不等于正常服务费或合法报酬。
The official refused the bribe.
那名官员拒绝了贿赂。
The employee was accused of accepting a bribe.
该员工被指控收受贿赂。
Nobody should have to bribe someone to receive a public service.
任何人都不应该靠行贿才能获得公共服务。

07. disbarment · 关键词 · 回看 00:37:16
美式 IPA：/dɪsˈbɑrmənt/
词性：noun
English definition: The removal of a lawyer's authorization to practice law.
取消律师执业资格
字幕中出现：disbarred
比暂时 suspension 更严重；动词 disbar。
The misconduct could lead to disbarment.
不当行为可能导致吊销律师资格。
He faced disbarment after falsifying records.
他因伪造记录面临取消执业资格。
Disbarment ended her legal practice.
取消资格终结了她的律师执业。

08. legitimate · 关键词 · 回看 00:24:49
美式 IPA：/ləˈdʒɪtəmət/
词性：adjective
English definition: Allowed by law or accepted rules; also genuine or reasonably justified.
合法正当的；真实合理的
字幕中出现：legitimate
legitimate concern 合理担忧；不局限于合法性。
They run a legitimate business.
他们经营合法生意。
That is a legitimate concern.
那是合理的担忧。
We checked whether the document was legitimate.
我们核查了文件是否真实有效。

09. in broad daylight · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:35:36
词性：adverb phrase
English definition: Openly during the day, often emphasizing how bold or surprising an action is.
光天化日之下
字幕中出现：in broad daylight
常带“竟然公开做坏事”的惊讶语气。
The robbery happened in broad daylight.
抢劫发生在光天化日之下。
They removed the equipment in broad daylight.
他们在大白天搬走了设备。
He couldn't believe the attack occurred in broad daylight.
他不敢相信袭击发生在大白天。
