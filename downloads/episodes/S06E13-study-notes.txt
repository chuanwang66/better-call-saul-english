英文梗概（80 words，含剧透）
After fleeing Marion's house, Gene is arrested. Saul uses negotiation and a carefully shaped story to obtain an extraordinarily favourable proposed sentence. Learning of Kim's confession changes the direction of his defence. At the hearing, he abandons the protective account and admits his role in Walt's criminal enterprise, along with the damage he caused Chuck. He accepts a much longer sentence and reclaims the name Jimmy McGill. Kim later visits him in prison, where they share a brief, quiet connection.
