英文梗概（75 words，含剧透）
Jimmy and Kim marry and agree on a new standard of honesty between them. Saul represents Lalo in a murder case, using information supplied through Mike to undermine the prosecution's evidence. The judge sets bail at seven million dollars. Gus and Nacho arrange an attack on a Los Pollos Hermanos restaurant to support the larger deception. Jimmy also lashes out at Howard, whose job offer touches unresolved feelings about Chuck, status and Jimmy's chosen identity.

重点词汇：12 项（单词与词组合计）
关键词采用常见美式读法的宽式 IPA；/ɝ/、/ɚ/ 表示带 r 色彩的元音，/i/、/u/ 不另加长音符号。音标对应显示的词条形式。词性列出本页讲解的用法；部分词有其他读法或词性。

01. bail · 关键词 · 回看 00:14:38
美式 IPA：/beɪl/
词性：noun / verb
English definition: Conditional release before trial, sometimes secured by money; to secure someone's release in this way.
保释；保释金
字幕中出现：bail
常用 on bail 或 post bail；保释不表示无罪。
The defendant was released on bail.
被告获准保释。
The family helped him post bail.
家人帮助他缴纳保释金。
The judge considered the request for bail.
法官审议了保释申请。

02. prosecution · 关键词 · 回看 00:40:31
美式 IPA：/ˌprɑsəˈkjuʃən/
词性：noun
English definition: The pursuit of criminal charges in court; also the lawyers bringing those charges.
控方；起诉；检控
字幕中出现：prosecution
the prosecution 常指刑事案件中的控方。
The prosecution presented its evidence.
控方出示了证据。
The defense challenged the prosecution's account.
辩方质疑了控方的说法。
The file was sent for prosecution.
案卷被移送起诉。

03. arson · 关键词 · 回看 00:08:25
美式 IPA：/ˈɑrsən/
词性：noun
English definition: The crime of deliberately and unlawfully setting property on fire.
纵火罪；故意纵火
字幕中出现：arson
通常为不可数名词，区别于一般意外失火。
Investigators suspected arson.
调查人员怀疑有人故意纵火。
He was charged with arson.
他被控纵火罪。
The report distinguished arson from an electrical fire.
报告区分了纵火和电气火灾。

04. waive · 关键词 · 回看 00:08:28
美式 IPA：/weɪv/
词性：verb
English definition: To voluntarily give up a right, requirement, claim, or fee.
自愿放弃权利、要求或费用
字幕中出现：waives
waive a right/fee；与 wave 挥手区分。
She waived her right to remain silent.
她放弃了保持沉默的权利。
The bank waived the fee.
银行免除了费用。
They refused to waive the requirement.
他们拒绝免除这项要求。

05. implicate · 关键词 · 回看 00:42:26
美式 IPA：/ˈɪmplɪˌkeɪt/
词性：verb
English definition: To connect someone to wrongdoing or suggest that they were involved.
牵连；暗示某人涉案
字幕中出现：implicating
implicate someone in a crime；暗示涉案不等于定罪。
The documents implicated a senior official.
文件牵连到一名高级官员。
He refused to implicate his partner.
他拒绝供出同伙。
New evidence implicated the contractor in the fraud.
新证据显示承包商可能涉及诈骗。

06. ephemeral · 关键词 · 回看 00:15:26
美式 IPA：/ɪˈfɛmɚəl/
词性：adjective
English definition: Existing or remaining significant for only a short time.
短暂的；转瞬即逝的
字幕中出现：Ephemeral
较正式，常用于名声、愉悦、事物存在的时间。
Online fame can be ephemeral.
网络名气可能转瞬即逝。
They sold an ephemeral sense of success.
他们兜售一种短暂的成功感。
The installation explores ephemeral beauty.
装置作品探索短暂的美。

07. testimony · 关键词 · 回看 00:42:26
美式 IPA：/ˈtɛstəˌmoʊni/
词性：noun
English definition: A witness's formal statement of facts, especially under oath.
证言；证词
字幕中出现：testimony
法律语境常不可数；指证人陈述，不等于所有证据。
The witness gave testimony in court.
证人在法庭上作了证。
Her testimony helped clarify the timeline.
她的证词帮助理清了时间线。
Was the testimony consistent with the records?
证词与记录一致吗？

08. allegation · 关键词 · 回看 00:40:13
美式 IPA：/ˌæləˈɡeɪʃən/
词性：noun
English definition: A claim of wrongdoing that has not yet been established as true.
尚未经证实的指控或说法
字幕中出现：allegation
allegation of misconduct；避免把 allegation 当作已确认事实。
The company denied the allegation.
公司否认了这项指控。
An allegation is not a conviction.
指控不等于定罪。
Investigators examined allegations of bribery.
调查人员审查了有关行贿的指控。

09. fabricate · 关键词 · 回看 00:42:26
美式 IPA：/ˈfæbrɪˌkeɪt/
词性：verb
English definition: To invent a false story or evidence; also to manufacture something.
捏造；制造
字幕中出现：fabricated
fabricate evidence/story 为贬义；fabricate parts 为中性制造。
He fabricated an excuse.
他编造了一个借口。
The witness denied fabricating evidence.
证人否认伪造证据。
The workshop fabricates metal components.
车间制造金属零件。

10. witness tampering · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:40:13
词性：noun phrase
English definition: Improperly influencing, threatening, or interfering with a witness or their testimony.
干扰、胁迫或不当影响证人
字幕中出现：witness tampering
法律术语；不是所有与证人接触都构成该行为。
The messages prompted a witness-tampering investigation.
这些信息引发了干扰证人的调查。
He denied any witness tampering.
他否认存在干扰证人行为。
The order warned against witness tampering.
命令警告不得干扰证人。

11. bad faith · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:12:22
词性：noun phrase
English definition: Dishonesty or an intention to mislead rather than deal fairly.
恶意；缺乏诚意
字幕中出现：bad faith
in bad faith 与 in good faith 相对；法律语境含义依具体规则。
They negotiated in bad faith.
他们在谈判中缺乏诚意。
The complaint alleged bad faith.
投诉指称对方存在恶意。
A disagreement alone does not prove bad faith.
仅有分歧不能证明恶意。

12. out on bail · 词组 / 术语 · 回看 00:14:38
词性：adjective / adverb phrase
English definition: Released from custody under bail conditions while a criminal case continues.
获准保释、在外候审
字幕中出现：out on bail
区别于被撤销指控；仍可能有条件或限制。
He was out on bail pending trial.
他获保释在外候审。
She contacted counsel while out on bail.
她保释期间联系了律师。
Being out on bail does not end the case.
保释在外并不意味着案件结束。
